## What Are Smoky Plum Cat Eye Nails?

Smoky plum cat eye nails are inspired by smoky evenings, plum flowers, velvet, and moonlight. The base is a deep plum mauve color. On top, there is a magnetic cat eye effect - a shiny silver-pink line that moves when you move your hand, just like a cat's eye.

On top of that, there is a transparent plum butterfly, tiny silver stars, and little pearls. Together, they look like a starry plum sky on your nails.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Plum Base

Apply a smoky plum cat eye gel polish on all nails. Do not cure yet.

## Step 3: Create Cat Eye Effect

Hold a cat eye magnet over each nail for 10 to 15 seconds. The magnet will pull the shimmer into a glowing line. Cure under UV light.

## Step 4: Add Butterfly

On the middle finger, place a transparent plum butterfly charm with nail glue. Add a tiny crystal in the center.

## Step 5: Add Stars And Pearls

Add tiny silver star charms and small pearls on the other nails with nail glue. Keep them near the cuticle for a clean look.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the cat eye shine like glass.

## How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with butterfly and star charms - do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a charm feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel cat eye polish. Gel will keep the cat eye glow perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
