# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Sheer Base

Apply a sheer lavender-pink polish on all nails. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Add Holographic Chrome

Rub holographic chrome powder softly on all nails for a rainbow iridescent finish. Apply a top coat and cure.

## Step 4: Add 3D Butterflies

Place clear 3D butterfly charms on each nail with nail glue. Press gently and hold for 10 seconds.

## Step 5: Add Silver Lines And Crystals

With a thin silver liner, draw delicate lines around the butterflies. Add tiny crystals and stars.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the butterflies. Cure one last time. This will make the holo shine like glass.

## How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with butterfly charms - do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a charm feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel holo chrome polish. Gel will keep the holographic glow perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
