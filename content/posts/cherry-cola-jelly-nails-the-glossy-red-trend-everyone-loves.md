If you love dark red nails but want something more juicy and modern, Cherry Cola Jelly Nails are for you. This look is like a glass of cherry cola on your nails - deep red, shiny, and see-through in the light. No wonder it is going viral everywhere on Pinterest and Instagram.

## What Are Cherry Cola Jelly Nails?

Cherry cola jelly nails are made with a sheer, jelly-like polish in a deep cherry-red shade. Unlike normal dark red polish, this one looks glassy and translucent. In sunlight, you can see a little glow from inside the nail - just like real cherry cola.

The best part is that this shade is not too bright and not too dark. It sits right in the middle, which is why it looks expensive and classy on everyone.

## Why Everyone Loves Them

1\. They look expensive and classy without trying too hard

2\. They suit all skin tones, from fair to deep

3\. They work on short nails, long nails, almond, square, and oval shapes

4\. Perfect for winter, Christmas, weddings, and date night

5\. Easy to do at home, you do not need a salon

## Which Shape Looks Best?

Almond shape looks the best for this design because it makes your fingers look long and slim. If your nails are short, do not worry. A short oval or soft square shape also looks very pretty with jelly polish. Just keep the length equal on all nails for a clean look.

## How To Get This Look At Home

You only need 4 things: base coat, cherry red jelly polish, glossy top coat, and a nail file.

## Step 1: Shape Your Nails

File your nails in almond or oval shape. Push back your cuticles gently and clean the nail surface.

## Step 2: Apply Base Coat

Apply one thin layer of clear base coat. This will protect your natural nails from staining and help the polish last longer. Let it dry for 2 minutes.

## Step 3: Apply Jelly Polish

Apply 2 thin coats of cherry red jelly polish. Do not apply one thick coat, it will look patchy. Let the first coat dry fully, then apply the second coat. You will see the juicy glassy color appear in the second coat.

## Step 4: Apply Top Coat

This is the most important step. Apply a super glossy top coat. This gives that wet, glass-like shine that makes cherry cola nails look so special. Let it dry for 5 minutes.

## Pro Tip If You Do Not Have Jelly Polish

Mix 1 drop of deep red polish + 1 drop of clear top coat on a small plate. Mix it well and apply it like normal polish. You will get the same jelly effect at home without buying a new polish.

## 3 Variations You Can Try

1\. Cherry Cola With Chrome: After the jelly polish, rub a little chrome powder on top for a shiny metallic look.

2\. Cherry Cola French: Keep the base clear and only apply cherry jelly on the tips for a modern French look.

3\. Cherry Cola With Glitter: Add one coat of fine red glitter on the ring finger for a party look.
