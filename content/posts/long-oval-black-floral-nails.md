**Oval nails** are the classic choice: rounded, balanced and naturally elegant. Grown long and painted glossy black with smoky flowers, they become something truly special, moody, luxurious and perfect for evenings out.

## What is an oval nail?

An oval nail has straight sides and a fully rounded tip, like an egg shape. It's softer than almond (no point) and more feminine than square.

## Why oval nails are so popular

- **Natural-looking.** Oval follows the shape of most cuticles.
- **Strong.** With no sharp corners or point, oval nails are less likely to snag or break.
- **Lengthening.** The rounded curve still makes fingers look longer.

## How to file oval nails

1. File the sides straight, following the natural sidewalls.
2. Round the tip in a smooth, even curve from one side to the other.
3. Make sure the highest point of the curve is in the center.
4. Buff the edge so it feels smooth.

## Recreate the black floral look

1. **Glossy black base:** two thin coats, then let it dry completely.
2. **Smoky flowers:** with a thin brush, paint five petals in grey or a sheer white over black, or use 3D flower charms.
3. **Pearl centers:** place a tiny pearl or gem in each flower.
4. **Gold details:** add thin gold vines or a curved gold line on accent nails.
5. **Top coat:** seal with a high-shine top coat.

## Styling tips

- Black nails look stunning with **gold rings** and cream or white outfits.
- Keep one or two nails **plain black** so the floral nails stand out.
- For a softer version, swap black for [burgundy](/nail-beauty/burgundy-wine-red-nails).

## Oval or almond?

Both shapes are flattering. Choose **oval** for strength and a natural look, or **almond** for extra length and drama. Compare them in our [almond nails guide](/nail-shapes/almond-nails-floral-gold-french).
