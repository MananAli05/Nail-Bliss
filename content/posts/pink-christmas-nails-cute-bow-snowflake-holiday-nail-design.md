Pink Christmas nails are the softest, cutest holiday nails this year. This pink design has pretty pink bows with pearls, a white snowflake, tiny rhinestones and a glittery pink Christmas tree.

Why You'll Love This Design

Every nail has a different soft pink design. One nail has a pink bow with a pearl, one has a snowflake with rhinestones, one has a pink glitter Christmas tree with pearls, and two are shiny pink with glitter. It is sweet, elegant and perfect for Christmas photos.

Perfect For

Christmas parties, Christmas Eve, family dinners, holiday photos, gift exchange and winter vacations. These nails look amazing with a white sweater, pink dress or cozy winter outfit.

How To Get This Look

Start with clean, shaped almond nails. Use soft pink polish for the base, pink glitter for the tree nail, and add pink bow charms with pearls. Add the hand-painted snowflake and Christmas tree, with tiny pearls and rhinestones. Finish with a glossy top coat.

Styling Tips

Pair these nails with a gold bow ring, a white knit sweater and soft pink makeup. These nails will be the star of all your Christmas pictures.

If you love soft, cute and elegant Christmas nails, this pink bow design is a must-try this holiday season.\\
