Candy Cane Christmas Nails are the cutest way to get into the holiday spirit. With classic red and white stripes, delicate snowflakes, tiny pearls, and a sweet red bow, this design looks festive, elegant, and so pretty in photos.

Why We Love This Design

This look is perfect if you want something festive but not too loud. The soft pink base keeps it elegant, while the candy cane tips add that fun Christmas touch. The snowflake art and pearl details make it look like a luxury salon manicure.

Design Details

\- Almond shape for a long, elegant look

\- Red and white candy cane French tips

\- White snowflake art on two nails

\- Tiny pearl accents for a soft glow

\- Red 3D bow with a pearl centre

\- Small candy cane charm on the ring finger

What To Ask At The Salon

Show your nail artist this photo and ask for almond-shaped nails with a nude pink base, red and white candy cane French tips, snowflake art, and pearl and bow charms. You can also ask for press-on nails if you want the same look at home.

Perfect For

Christmas parties, family dinners, New Year celebrations, holiday photos, and winter weddings. This design goes beautifully with a white sweater, red dress, or any festive outfit.

Pro Tip

Keep the base glossy and use a top coat every 3-4 days to keep the candy cane stripes looking fresh all holiday season.
