# What Are Smoky Mocha Aura Nails?

Smoky mocha aura nails are inspired by mocha coffee, smoky evenings, candlelight, and gold jewelry. The base is a sheer nude that fades into a deep smoky mocha brown in the center, like an aura glowing from inside the nail.

On top, there is a glossy glass finish with tiny gold stars and crystals scattered like sparkles in a coffee cup. Together, they look like your nails are made of warm mocha glass with gold dust.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Sheer Nude Base

Apply a sheer nude polish on all nails. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Create Mocha Aura

With a sponge or aura brush, dab deep mocha brown polish in the center of each nail. Blend the edges softly to create a smoky aura effect. Cure.

## Step 4: Add Glossy Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat for a glass effect. Cure.

## Step 5: Add Gold Stars And Crystals

Place tiny gold stars and small crystals on each nail with nail glue. Place one crystal near the cuticle for a jewelry look.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a final glossy top coat carefully around the charms. Cure one last time. This will make the mocha aura shine like glass.

# How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the aura shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel aura polish. Gel will keep the mocha aura perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
