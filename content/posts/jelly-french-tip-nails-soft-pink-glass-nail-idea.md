## What Are Jelly French Tip Nails?

Jelly French Tip Nails are a modern version of the classic French manicure. Instead of a solid white tip on a solid pink base, this design uses a clear jelly base and a soft pink jelly tip.

Jelly nails are sheer, see-through nails that look like tinted glass or jelly. They are glossy, juicy, and very dreamy. They are not a solid color, they are translucent, so you can see a little bit of your natural nail through them.

In this photo, the base is a clear, soft nude jelly, and the tips are a soft dusty pink jelly. The tips are not a hard line, they are softly blended, like watercolor. On two nails, there is a tiny crystal and a thin gold chain detail. On one nail, there is a tiny crystal butterfly, and on another nail, there is a tiny rose-gold heart.

Together, the clear base, the pink tips, and the tiny crystals create a look that is soft, elegant, and absolutely stunning. It is simple but still special.

## The Design Breakdown - What Makes This Look So Special

Let us look at each part of this design in detail so you know exactly what to ask your nail artist for.

## 1. The Shape - Long Almond

The nails in this photo are long almond shaped. Almond is one of the most flattering shapes because it makes your fingers look longer and slimmer. It is also perfect for French tips because it shows off the tip beautifully. If you prefer shorter nails, don't worry. This design also looks beautiful on short almond or oval nails. Just ask for a thinner pink tip.

## 2. The Base Color - Clear Jelly Nude

The base color is a clear, sheer nude jelly. It is not a solid pink, it is translucent like glass. This is what makes jelly nails so special - they look glossy, juicy, and dreamy. The base is very natural, like your real nail but with a little bit of shine and pink glow. For the best result, ask for a sheer nude jelly gel polish with a high-gloss finish.

## 3. The Tips - Soft Pink Jelly

The tips are a soft, dusty pink jelly. They are not bright pink, they are muted, like rose pink or mauve pink. The pink is translucent, not solid, so it blends softly into the clear base. This gives that dreamy, watercolor effect. The tip is slightly curved and follows the natural shape of the nail. It is elegant, soft, and very feminine.

## 4. The Crystal and Gold Details

On the thumb and pinky nails, there is a tiny crystal with a thin gold chain at the base of the tip. The chain looks like delicate jewelry. On the index nail, there is a tiny crystal butterfly that sparkles in the light. On the middle nail, there is a tiny rose-gold heart. These details are very small, but they make the whole manicure look expensive and luxurious.

## 5. The Glossy Finish

The whole manicure has a super glossy, glass-like finish. It looks wet, shiny, and fresh. This is what makes jelly nails look so juicy. The gloss catches the light and makes your hands look healthy and polished.

## How To Get This Look At The Salon

When you go to the salon, show your nail artist this exact photo. Tell them you want long almond nails, clear jelly nude base, soft pink jelly French tips, and tiny crystals, gold chain, butterfly, and heart details. A good nail artist will be able to create the jelly effect with sheer gel polish and place the tiny crystals with nail glue.

If you want to make it last longer, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the shine for 2 to 3 weeks without chipping. Also ask for a strong top coat to protect the crystals.

## How To Recreate It At Home

If you love doing your nails at home, you can try a simpler version. You will need a clear jelly nude polish, a soft pink jelly polish, a thin brush, small crystals, and top coat. Start with a base coat, then apply 2 coats of clear jelly nude. Use the thin brush to paint the tips in soft pink jelly. Add tiny crystals with nail glue. Finish with top coat. It takes a little patience, but the result is so worth it.

## Tips To Make Your Jelly French Tip Nails Last Longer

To keep your manicure looking fresh, always wear gloves when washing dishes, apply cuticle oil every day, avoid using your nails as tools, and be gentle with the crystals. Reapply top coat every 4 to 5 days to keep the shine and protect the art.
