# Dark Romance Nails - Burgundy Lace Gothic Nail Design

Dark romance nails are the most moody, romantic, and luxurious nails for gothic lovers. This design mixes deep burgundy wine, black lace, crystal cross, and tiny diamonds to create a look that feels like a dark romance novel come to life. It is deep, elegant, and so mysterious. If you love burgundy, lace, and dark romance vibes, these nails are made for you.

# What Are Dark Romance Nails?

Dark romance nails are inspired by dark romance novels, burgundy roses, black lace, vintage jewelry, and candlelight. The base is a sheer nude that fades into a deep burgundy wine tip, like dark red wine.

On top, there is delicate black lace art like vintage lace fabric, with a gold crystal cross and tiny diamonds. Together, they look like your nails are made of burgundy velvet and black lace jewelry.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long stiletto shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Nude To Burgundy Ombre

Apply a sheer nude polish. With a sponge, dab deep burgundy on the tips to create an ombre effect. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Paint Black Lace

With a thin black liner or lace stamp, paint delicate lace patterns over the nails. Let it dry.

## Step 4: Add Crystal Cross

Place a gold crystal cross charm on the middle finger with nail glue. Add tiny diamonds on the other nails.

## Step 5: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the charms. Cure one last time. This will make the burgundy shine like glass.

## Styling Tips For Dark Romance Nails

Pair these nails with a burgundy velvet dress, black lace sleeve, and crystal cross ring. Add dark red roses and gold bracelet for the full dark romance look.

For makeup, keep it dark and glowing. Deep burgundy lips, smoky eyes, glowing skin, and gold highlighter. This will match your nails perfectly.

For hair, dark waves, a low bun, or a braid with a black lace bow will look beautiful with these nails.

## How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with cross charm - do not pick at it. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a charm feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel ombre polish. Gel will keep the burgundy perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
