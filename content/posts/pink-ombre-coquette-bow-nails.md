The **coquette** trend is all about soft pinks, ribbons and romance, and nothing captures it better than **bow nails**. This look starts with a dreamy pink-to-white ombré, then adds bows three different ways for a mix of textures.

## Start with a soft pink ombré

1. Apply a base coat and one sheer pink coat.
2. Lightly sponge **white or very pale pink** onto the tips.
3. Build the fade with two or three layers, then smooth it with a top coat.

## 3 ways to add coquette bows

### 1. Painted bows (flat and delicate)

With a thin brush and pink polish, paint two small loops that meet in the center, then two thin ribbon tails. Add a tiny rhinestone in the middle as the "knot."

### 2. 3D gel bows (raised and dimensional)

Use thick **builder gel or 3D gel** to sculpt a bow shape on the nail, cure it under an LED lamp, then paint it pink. These have a soft, puffy look, like real satin ribbon.

### 3. Bow charms (quickest)

Press a small **metal or acrylic bow charm** into a dot of gel or top coat. Gold bows look extra elegant.

## Finishing touches

- Add a **heart-shaped gem** on one nail.
- Paint a tiny bow on the **pinky** to balance the hand.
- Seal with a glossy top coat, carefully around 3D details.

## Tips

- Mix bow styles across your nails for a playful, curated look.
- Keep the base soft so the bows are the star.
- A **bow ring** completes the coquette look.
