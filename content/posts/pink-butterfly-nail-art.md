**Butterfly nails** are pure whimsy: soft, feminine and perfect for spring and summer. This look pairs delicate pink butterflies with milky ombré tips and tiny silver sparkles for a dreamy, fairytale finish.

## Three ways to add butterflies

| Method | Skill level | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nail stickers or decals | Beginner | Quick, perfect results |
| Hand-painting | Intermediate | A unique, artistic look |
| 3D charms | Beginner | Extra dimension and sparkle |

## Step 1: The ombré base

1. Apply a base coat and one coat of sheer pink.
2. Dab a little **white polish on a makeup sponge** and press it onto the tips.
3. Repeat once or twice to build a soft fade.
4. Add a coat of top coat to smooth out the gradient.

## Step 2: Add the butterflies

**With stickers:** use tweezers to place a butterfly decal on two or three nails, then press down the edges.

**Hand-painted:**

1. With pink polish and a thin brush, paint two teardrop shapes on each side of a center line for the wings.
2. Add two smaller wings below.
3. Paint a thin darker line down the middle for the body.
4. Add tiny white highlights on the wings for dimension.

## Step 3: Sparkles and finishing

Dot tiny **silver stars** or small rhinestones around the butterflies, then seal everything with a glossy top coat.

## Styling tips

- Keep butterflies on **accent nails** so the design feels light.
