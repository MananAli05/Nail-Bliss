**Glazed donut nails** became famous for one reason: they make nails look like they're glowing. A sheer pink base topped with a pearly chrome sheen creates a soft, lit-from-within finish that's elegant enough for every day and pretty enough for special occasions.

## What are glazed donut nails?

The look is a **sheer milky or pink base** with a thin layer of **white pearl chrome powder** on top. The chrome adds a glossy, almost wet shimmer, like the glaze on a donut.

## How to get glazed donut nails (with gel)

1. Shape nails into a soft **almond** or **oval**.
2. Apply a gel base and two coats of **sheer pink** gel, curing each layer.
3. Apply a **no-wipe top coat** and cure.
4. Lightly rub **white pearl chrome powder** over the nail. A little goes a long way.
5. Remove excess powder and seal with another no-wipe top coat.

## Without gel

Use a sheer pink polish, then apply a **pearl or iridescent shimmer top coat**. It's softer than chrome powder but still gives a beautiful glaze.

## Cute ways to style it

- Add a **tiny heart charm** or pearl near the cuticle on one nail.
- Try a **lilac** version for a cooler tone; see our [lilac chrome nails](/nail-designs/lilac-chrome-nails).
- Add soft **coquette bows** on top for a girly twist.

## Why it's a perfect everyday manicure

- **Grows out gracefully.** The sheer base hides regrowth.
- **Matches everything.** Gold, silver, casual or dressy.
- **Makes nails look healthy.** The glow hides small imperfections.
