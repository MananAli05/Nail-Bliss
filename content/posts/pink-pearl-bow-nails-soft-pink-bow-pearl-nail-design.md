# Pink Pearl Bow Nails - Soft Pink Bow Pearl Nail Design

Pink pearl bow nails are the most cute, soft, and coquette nails for pink lovers. This design mixes soft baby pink, delicate 3D bows, tiny pearls, and small crystals to create a look that feels like a pink ribbon gift box. It is sweet, elegant, and so feminine. # What Are Pink Pearl Bow Nails?

Pink pearl bow nails are inspired by pink ribbons, pearls, lace, and soft romance. The base is a soft milky pink with a hint of glitter that sparkles in the light.

On top, there are small white-pink 3D bows with a pearl in the center, tiny pearls in a curved line, and a small crystal star. Together, they look like tiny pink gift bows on your nails.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Pink Base

Apply a soft milky pink polish with fine glitter on all nails. Apply 2 thin layers. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Add 3D Bows

Place small pink-white 3D bow charms on the middle and thumb nails with nail glue. Add a tiny pearl in the center of each bow.

## Step 4: Paint Bow Outlines

With a thin white brush, paint tiny bow outlines on the other nails. Add a tiny crystal in the center.

## Step 5: Add Pearls

Place tiny white pearls in a curved line near the cuticle on each nail with nail glue.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the charms. Cure one last time. This will make the nails shine like glass.

# How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with bow charms - do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a bow feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the bows and pearls perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
