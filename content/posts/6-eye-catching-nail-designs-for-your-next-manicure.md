If you cannot decide what to get for your next manicure, this post will solve it. Here are 6 Eye-Catching Nail Designs in one gorgeous plum color. All 6 are different, all 6 are beautiful, and one of them will be perfect for you.

Plum is the perfect color for this season. It is dark like winter, but soft like spring. It looks classy in the office and glam at a party. And when you add floral, bows, glitter, or French tips, it becomes even more stunning.

## Design 1: Plum Floral Nails

This is the most romantic design in the collage. Deep plum nails with one accent nail that has a big purple flower on a nude base. The flower looks hand-painted and so delicate.

This design is perfect for weddings, engagements, and spring parties. If you love soft, feminine nails, pick this one. You can ask your nail artist for one big flower on the ring finger only, and keep the rest plain plum.

## Design 2: Plum Ombre With Crystal Bows

This is the most viral design right now. Plum ombre nails that fade from light pink to deep plum, with tiny crystal bows on top.

The ombre makes your nails look long, and the crystal bows make them look like jewelry. This design is perfect if you want something cute and glam at the same time. It is also great for birthdays and photos.

## Design 3: Plum Glitter With Leaf Art

If you love sparkle, this one is for you. Deep plum nails mixed with full glitter nails and one leaf art accent nail.

The glitter catches light in every photo, and the leaf design keeps it elegant, not too heavy. This is perfect for winter, Christmas parties, and night events.

## Design 4: Plum French With Hearts

This is a modern twist on French nails. Nude base with deep plum French tips, tiny hearts, and one glitter accent nail.

If you like French nails but want something more fun, this is the one. The hearts make it so cute for Valentine's Day, anniversaries, and date nights. And it still looks clean and classy for everyday.

## Design 5: Plum Bow Nails

This is the simplest but most classy design. Deep glossy plum nails with one accent nail that has a plum bow and a small crystal in the middle.

Bows are trending everywhere right now - on clothes, on hair, and on nails. This design is perfect if you want a coquette, soft, girly look. It is great for bridal, baby showers, and brunch.

## Design 6: Plum Dainty Flowers

The last design is the cutest. Light pink base with tiny plum flowers and small crystals on most nails, plus one full glitter plum nail.

This is perfect for short nails because the small flowers do not need a lot of space. If you want something sweet and easy to wear daily, pick this one.

## Which Design Should You Pick?

Pick Design 1 or 5 if you have a wedding or formal event.

Pick Design 2 or 3 if you love glam and glitter.

Pick Design 4 if you love French nails with a twist.

Pick Design 6 if you want something cute for everyday.

## How To Make Them Last Longer

With gel polish, all these plum designs last 2 to 3 weeks. Always apply a base coat first so plum polish does not stain your nails. Apply cuticle oil every night and wear gloves while washing dishes. If you have glitter nails, apply 2 layers of top coat so the glitter stays smooth.

## Can I Do These At Home?

Yes, Design 4 and Design 5 are the easiest to do at home. For Design 2 and Design 6, you will need small brushes and crystal stickers. For the best result, show this collage photo to your nail artist and ask her to copy the design you like.

## FAQ

## Q: Does plum color suit all skin tones?

Yes, plum suits fair, medium, and deep skin tones. It looks especially beautiful on medium to deep skin.

## Q: Are these designs good for short nails?

Yes, Design 4 and Design 6 look beautiful on short nails too. For short nails, keep the art smaller and simpler.

## Q: Which design is best for winter?

Design 3 with glitter is the best for winter and Christmas parties.

## Final Thoughts

These 6 plum nail designs prove that one color can give you 6 totally different looks - floral, glam, cute, classy, and romantic. Save this post, pick your favorite, and show it to your nail artist for your next manicure. Which one is your favorite? 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6?\\
