## 6 Eye-Catching Plum Nail Designs For Your Next Manicure

Plum is that perfect colour that sits between bold and elegant. It is not as loud as red, not as dark as black, and not as soft as pink. It is rich, warm, feminine, and powerful at the same time.

If you want nails that look expensive, classy, and eye-catching without trying too hard, plum is the answer. In this article, we have collected 6 stunning plum nail designs that you can try for your next manicure. Each design is different. One is floral, one is ombre, one is French, one is glitter, one is bow, and one is tiny flowers.

Save your favourite, show it to your nail artist, and get ready for compliments.

## Why Plum Nails Are So Popular Right Now

Plum nails are trending because they work for every season and every occasion.

In winter, plum looks cozy with sweaters and shawls. In summer, plum looks fresh with white and pastel dresses. For weddings, plum looks royal and traditional. For office, plum looks professional but still stylish. For parties, plum with glitter and rhinestones looks glamorous.

Plum also suits all skin tones. On fair skin, it looks deep and romantic. On medium skin, it looks warm and glowing. On dark skin, it looks bold and luxurious.

Another reason plum is loved is that it pairs beautifully with gold jewellery, pearl rings, nude makeup, and both Eastern and Western dresses.

## How To Choose The Right Plum Design For You

Before we start, here is a quick guide.

If you love simple nails, choose Design 1 or Design 5. If you love cute nails, choose Design 4 or Design 6. If you love glamorous nails, choose Design 2 or Design 3. If you have short nails, choose French, ombre, or tiny flowers. If you have long nails, choose big flower, bow, or full glitter.

## How To Make Your Plum Manicure Last Longer

Follow these simple tips to keep your nails fresh for 2 to 3 weeks.

Always apply a good base coat and top coat. Do not use nails as tools to open things. Wear gloves while washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil daily. Avoid very hot water for long time. Do not bite or pick your nails. Get a refill after 2 weeks if you have gel or acrylic nails.

## DIY Vs Salon - Which Is Better

**DIY At Home**

DIY is good for simple plum glossy nails. You can use plum polish with top coat. Use dotting tool for tiny flowers and hearts. Use nail stickers for bows and leaves if you are a beginner.

**Salon**

Salon is best for ombre, hand-painted flowers, and rhinestone work. Gel polish lasts longer and looks more glossy. Nail artist can create clean, detailed art.

If you want the designs in this article exactly like the photos, salon is the best option. Show the photo to your artist and ask for the same shape and finish.

## What To Ask Your Nail Artist

Copy this list and take it to your appointment.

I want plum colour, not maroon, not purple. I want glossy finish, not matte. I want almond or oval shape. I want one accent nail with art, others simple. I want small rhinestones, not big stones. Please use gel polish for long-lasting shine. Please seal the design with top coat.

This will help your artist understand exactly what you want.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. Are plum nails good for short nails?

Yes, plum looks beautiful on short nails too. French, tiny flowers, and glossy plum are best for short nails. They make short nails look neat and elegant.

### Q2. Which plum shade is best for fair skin?

Deep plum with a little red undertone looks best on fair skin. It adds warmth and makes hands glow.

### Q3. Which plum shade is best for dark skin?

Rich, dark plum with a glossy finish looks stunning on dark skin. It looks bold, luxurious, and very classy.

### Q4. How long do plum gel nails last?

Gel plum nails usually last 2 to 3 weeks with proper care. Regular polish lasts 5 to 7 days.

### Q5. Can I wear plum nails in office?

Yes, absolutely. Choose simple glossy plum or leaf design for office. Avoid too much glitter and big rhinestones for a professional look.

### Q6. Are plum nails good for weddings?

Yes, plum is one of the best colours for weddings. It looks royal with both Eastern and Western bridal dresses. Big flower and ombre bow designs are perfect for brides.

### Q7. What colour dress matches plum nails?

Plum nails match with plum, maroon, black, white, cream, beige, gold, pink, and lavender dresses. It is a very versatile colour.

### Q8. Can I do plum nails at home?

Yes, you can do simple plum nails at home with plum polish, base coat, and top coat. For flower, bow, and ombre art, salon is better.

### Q9. Do plum nails make hands look fair?

Yes, deep plum creates a contrast that makes hands look brighter and cleaner, especially with a glossy finish.

### Q10. Which design is most trending right now?

Plum bow nails and plum ombre bow nails are the most trending right now on Instagram and Pinterest.
