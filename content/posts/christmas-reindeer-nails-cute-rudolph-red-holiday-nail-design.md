## How To Do Christmas Reindeer Nails At Home - Step By Step

You can do Christmas reindeer nails at home if you have patience and the right tools. Here is the full process for this exact design.

## What You Need:

Soft pink nude polish, sparkly red polish, deep red polish, white polish, brown polish, black polish, gold polish, thin nail art brush, dotting tool, top coat, base coat, small white bow charm, small pearl, cuticle oil

### Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Remove old polish. File your nails into a long almond shape like in the photo. Push back cuticles gently. Clean nails well. Apply base coat and let it dry.

### Step 2: Apply Base Colors

For thumb and pinky, apply 2 coats of sparkly red polish. For index and ring finger, apply 2 coats of soft pink nude polish. For middle finger, apply 2 coats of deep red polish. Let each coat dry completely.

### Step 3: Create The Reindeer Face

This is the most important step. On the index finger, use a thin brush to draw two brown antlers on the top sides. Draw two small black curved lines for closed happy eyes. Add two small pink dots for rosy cheeks. Add a big red dot in the center for the nose. Add a small gold star on top and tiny white dots for snow. Let it dry. Take your time, the reindeer face needs patience.

### Step 4: Create The Snowflakes

On the thumb and pinky nails, draw a big white snowflake in the center with a thin brush. Draw 6 lines from the center like a star. Add small branches on each line. Add tiny white dots around it for sparkle. On the ring finger, draw a red snowflake in the center on the pink base. Add two tiny red hearts on top and one small red heart below with a thin gold line.

### Step 5: Create The Sweater Texture And Bow

On the middle finger, use a thin brush to draw vertical lines on the red base to create a sweater texture. Make the lines close together and neat. Let it dry. Then stick a small white bow charm in the center with nail glue. Add a small pearl in the center of the bow.

### Step 6: Apply Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails to seal the design and add shine. Be careful around the bow. Apply top coat gently so the bow does not move. Let it dry completely.

### Step 7: Apply Cuticle Oil

Massage cuticle oil around nails. This makes your manicure look fresh and salon-perfect.

And done! Your Christmas reindeer nails are ready.

## Pro Tips For Perfect Christmas Reindeer Nails

1\. Use a thin brush for the reindeer face and snowflakes. Thick brushes will make the design messy.

2\. Let each layer dry before adding the next one. If you rush, the colors will mix.

3\. Use stickers for snowflakes if you cannot draw them. Stickers look just as beautiful.

4\. Use nail glue for the bow and pearl, so they do not fall off.

5\. Always seal with top coat. Top coat makes the design last longer and look glossy.

6\. Work in good light. Small details like eyes and antlers need good light.

## How To Make Christmas Reindeer Nails Last Longer

With gel polish, Christmas reindeer nails can last 2 to 3 weeks. With regular polish, they can last 7 to 10 days.

Always use base coat and top coat. Wear gloves while washing dishes and cleaning. Do not use nails as tools. Be careful with the bow and pearl, do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil daily. Apply top coat again after 4 to 5 days for extra protection. Do not peel off polish.

If the bow or pearl falls off, you can stick it back with a small dot of nail glue and top coat.
