Christmas reindeer nails are the cutest holiday nails this year. This red design has a sweet Rudolph face, white snowflakes, a white bow with a pearl and a cozy sweater pattern.

Why You'll Love This Design

Every nail has a different Christmas design. One nail has a smiling Rudolph with a red nose, one has a white bow on deep red sweater texture, one has red snowflakes and hearts, and two have white snowflakes on glittery red. It is cute, festive and perfect for Christmas photos.

Perfect For

Christmas parties, Christmas Eve, family dinners, holiday photos, gift exchange and winter vacations. These nails look amazing with a white sweater, red dress or cozy winter outfit.

How To Get This Look

Start with clean, shaped almond nails. Use red glitter polish for the snowflake nails, soft nude for the Rudolph nail, and deep red for the bow nail. Add the Rudolph face, white bow charm and hand-painted snowflakes. Finish with a glossy top coat.

Styling Tips

Pair these nails with a gold bow ring, a white knit sweater and red lipstick. These nails will be the star of all your Christmas pictures.

If you love cute, festive and cozy Christmas nails, this reindeer design is a must-try this holiday season.\\
