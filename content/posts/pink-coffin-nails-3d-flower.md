**Coffin nails**, also called **ballerina nails**, are long nails that taper toward the tip and finish with a flat edge, like a ballet slipper. They're bold, modern and a gorgeous canvas for detailed nail art like 3D flowers and pearls.

## What makes coffin nails different?

Coffin combines a tapered shape with a **straight, flat tip**. It looks dramatic from far away, yet the flat edge is less likely to snag than a sharp stiletto point.

## Who should try coffin nails?

- Anyone who loves **long nails** and a trendy look
- People who want a **statement manicure** for weddings, parties or photos
- Those with long nail beds or acrylic or gel extensions (natural nails need good length)

## How to shape coffin nails

1. Start with long nails, either natural or extensions.
2. File both sides inward at an angle so they taper toward the tip.
3. File the tip **straight across** to make a flat edge.
4. Keep the tip narrower than the base, but not too thin, or it will break.
5. Smooth all edges.

## How to get this soft pink design

1. **Base:** sheer milky pink on all nails.
2. **French accent:** soft white tips outlined with a thin gold line on two nails.
3. **3D flower:** place a white 3D flower with a crystal center on the ring finger.
4. **Gold bow:** add a dainty gold bow charm on one nail.
5. **Pearls:** scatter a few tiny pearls near the tip of the pinky.
6. **Seal:** finish with top coat, building a little extra around charms so they stay put.

## Keep coffin nails strong

- Don't let them get too long. The longer the nail, the more leverage when you knock it.
- Use a **strengthening base coat** or gel builder.
- Use your fingertips, not your nails, to open things.
