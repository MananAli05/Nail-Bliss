# Emerald Gemstone Nails - Green Jewel Emerald Nail Design

#

Emerald gemstone nails are the most royal, luxurious, and glamorous nails for jewel lovers. This design mixes deep emerald green, real-looking gemstones, thin gold lines, tiny crystals, and green leaves to create a look that feels like wearing emerald jewelry on your nails. It is rich, elegant, and so royal. If you love emeralds, green, and luxury, these nails are made for you.

# What Are Emerald Gemstone Nails?

Emerald gemstone nails are inspired by emerald stones, royal jewelry, green velvet, and winter palaces. The base is a deep emerald green with a glossy, jewel-like shine. Some nails are sheer nude with emerald green marble swirls.

On top, there is a big emerald gemstone in the center, surrounded by tiny crystals like a ring. Thin gold lines wrap around the nails like gold veins in a gemstone. Together, they look like your nails are made of real emeralds.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Emerald Base

Apply a deep emerald green gel polish on 2-3 nails. On the other nails, apply a sheer nude polish. Cure under UV light.

## Step 3: Create Marble Swirls

With a thin brush, swirl emerald green and gold glitter on the nude nails to create a marble effect. Cure it.

## Step 4: Add Gold Lines

With a thin gold liner, draw delicate swirling gold lines on all nails.

## Step 5: Add Emerald Gemstone

On the ring finger, place a big emerald gemstone charm in the center with nail glue. Surround it with tiny crystals.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the gemstone. Cure one last time. This will make the nails shine like real jewels.

# How To Make Them Last Longer

#

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with the gemstone - do not pick at it. Apply cuticle oil every night. If the gem feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the emerald shine perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
