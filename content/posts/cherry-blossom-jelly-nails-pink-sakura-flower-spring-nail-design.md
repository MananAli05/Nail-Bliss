Cherry blossom jelly nails are the most soft, dreamy, and romantic nails for spring. This design mixes clear pink jelly, delicate sakura flowers, tiny silver branches, and falling petals to create a look that feels like spring rain under a cherry blossom tree. It is light, fresh, and so beautiful. If you love sakura, pink, and spring, these nails are made for you.

What Are Cherry Blossom Jelly Nails?

Cherry blossom jelly nails are inspired by Japanese sakura, spring gardens, and soft pink petals. The base is a clear pink jelly that is see-through like glass. You can see your natural nail through it, which makes it look fresh and clean.

On top, there are tiny hand-painted sakura flowers in soft pink and white with tiny crystal centers. Thin silver branches and falling petals float inside the jelly like they are frozen in water. It looks like a tiny spring garden on your nails.

How To Get This Look At Home

Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

Step 2: Apply Jelly Base

Apply a sheer pink jelly polish on all nails. Apply 2 thin layers for a glassy, see-through look. Cure under UV light if using gel.

Step 3: Paint Sakura Flowers

With a thin brush, paint tiny sakura flowers in soft pink and white. Add 5 petals to each flower. Add a tiny crystal in the center of each flower.

Step 4: Add Branches And Petals

With a thin silver liner, paint tiny branches around the flowers. Add small pink petals falling down the nail.

Step 5: Add Glitter

Add a tiny bit of fine silver glitter inside the jelly for a dewy sparkle. Keep it light and natural.

Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy jelly top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the nails shine like glass.

How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the jelly shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel jelly polish. Gel will keep the sakura flowers perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
