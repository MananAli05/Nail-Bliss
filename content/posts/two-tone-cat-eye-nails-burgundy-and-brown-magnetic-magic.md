If you cannot decide between burgundy and brown, Two Tone Cat Eye Nails are for you. This look mixes both colors in one manicure with a glowing magnetic shine. In one light it looks burgundy, in another light it looks brown. It is like two manicures in one.

## What Are Two Tone Cat Eye Nails?

Two tone cat eye nails are made with two different magnetic gel polishes - usually one burgundy and one brown. Some nails are painted burgundy, some are painted brown, and some nails have both colors blended together. A magnet is used to create the glowing cat eye line on top.

The result is warm, cozy, and very expensive looking. Perfect for fall and winter when you want dark nails but not plain black.

## Why Everyone Loves Them

1\. Two colors in one manicure, so it never looks boring

2\. The magnetic glow changes with light and movement

3\. Burgundy and brown suit all skin tones

4\. Perfect for fall, winter, weddings, and cozy sweater weather

5\. Looks classy in office and glam at parties

## Which Shape Looks Best?

Almond shape looks the best for this design because it makes the color shift look longer and more dramatic. The pointed shape also makes your fingers look slim. If your nails are short, a soft oval shape also works. Just keep all nails the same length for a clean two tone look.

## How To Get This Look At Home

You need: dark base coat, burgundy cat eye polish, brown cat eye polish, a cat eye magnet, and a glossy top coat. You will also need a UV lamp if you use gel polish.

## Step 1: Shape Your Nails

File your nails in almond shape. Push back your cuticles gently and clean the nail surface. Apply one layer of base coat and cure it.

## Step 2: Paint Two Tones

Paint your thumb, middle finger, and pinky in burgundy cat eye polish. Paint your index and ring finger in brown cat eye polish. This alternate pattern is what makes the two tone look so pretty.

## Step 3: Use The Magnet

Before curing, hold the magnet close to the nail for 10 to 15 seconds without touching it. You will see the glowing line appear. For burgundy nails, hold the magnet straight for a center line. For brown nails, hold it sideways for a diagonal line. This mix of lines makes the design look more interesting.

## Step 4: Cure And Top Coat

Cure each nail for 60 seconds under the lamp. Then apply a super glossy top coat on all nails and cure again. The top coat will seal the magnetic effect and make it look like glass.

## Pro Tip For Perfect Two Tone Blend

If you want a blended look like in the photo, apply burgundy polish on half the nail and brown polish on the other half, then blend the middle with a small brush before using the magnet. The colors will melt into each other and create a beautiful ombre cat eye effect.

## 3 Variations You Can Try

1\. Classic Two Tone: Alternate burgundy and brown nails, no blending.

2\. Blended Ombre: Mix both colors on every nail for a smooth gradient.

3\. Accent Two Tone: 4 nails in one color, 1 ring finger in the second color for a simple accent.

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

Do not apply thick coats, they will bubble and hide the magnetic effect. Do not use the magnet too far away, the line will be weak. Do not cure before using the magnet, the magnet only works on wet polish. And do not do all 5 nails at once - do one nail, magnet, cure, then move to the next.

## How To Make Them Last Longer

With gel polish, two tone cat eye nails last 2 to 3 weeks. Apply cuticle oil every night and wear gloves while washing dishes. If the shine becomes dull after a week, apply one fresh layer of top coat to bring back the glow.

## Who Should Try This Look?

If you love fall colors, if you wear brown sweaters and burgundy lipstick, or if you want a dark manicure that is not boring black - try two tone cat eye nails once. They look cozy, classy, and they get compliments in every light.

## FAQ

## Q: Can I do two tone cat eye nails without gel polish?

Magnetic effect works best with gel polish. Normal polish does not have strong magnetic particles.

## Q: Are two tone nails good for short nails?

Yes, they look beautiful on short nails too. Just use a thinner magnetic line.

## Q: Which two colors go best together?

Burgundy + brown, black + purple, and green + gold are the most popular two tone combos.
