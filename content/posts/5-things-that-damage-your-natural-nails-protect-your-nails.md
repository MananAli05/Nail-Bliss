# 5 Things That Damage Your Natural Nails - Protect Your Nails

Your natural nails are delicate. If they keep breaking, peeling, or feeling weak, you might be doing some small things every day that damage them without knowing. The good news is, all of these are easy to fix. Here are the 5 things that damage your natural nails the most, and what to do instead

## 1. Peeling Off Gel Or Acrylic Nails

This is the number 1 reason nails get damaged. When you peel off gel or acrylic, you also peel off the top layer of your natural nail. That leaves your nails thin, weak, and peeling.

What To Do Instead: Always soak off gel with acetone or go to a salon for safe removal. Never pick, peel, or bite off your gel nails.

## 2. Using Your Nails As Tools

Opening cans, scratching stickers, or opening boxes with your nails puts a lot of pressure on them. This causes them to bend, crack, and break from the tip.

What To Do Instead: Use your fingertips, a spoon, or a key to open things. Treat your nails like jewelry, not tools.

## 3. Skipping Base Coat And Cuticle Oil

If you apply polish directly on bare nails, the color stains your nails and makes them dry. And if you never use cuticle oil, your nails become dry, brittle, and break easily.

What To Do Instead: Always apply a base coat before color. And apply cuticle oil every night before sleeping. It keeps your nails soft, flexible, and strong.

## 4. Over-Filing And Wrong Filing

Filing your nails back and forth like a saw creates tiny cracks on the edges. Over-filing the top of the nail also makes it thin and weak. Filing when nails are wet makes it even worse.

What To Do Instead: Always file in one direction only, from side to center. Use a soft glass file, not a rough metal file. And always file when nails are dry.

## 5. Constant Water And Harsh Chemicals

Washing dishes without gloves, using bleach, or using nail polish remover with strong acetone every week dries out your nails. Water makes nails swell and then shrink, which causes peeling and splitting.

What To Do Instead: Always wear gloves when washing dishes or cleaning. Use acetone-free remover when you can. And wash your hands with a gentle soap.

How To Repair Already Damaged Nails

If your nails are already damaged, do not panic. It takes about 3 to 6 months for a nail to fully grow out, but you will see improvement in 2 to 3 weeks.

Take a break from gel and acrylic for 2 to 3 weeks. Keep your nails short and shaped. Apply cuticle oil twice a day. Use a nail strengthener 2 times a week. Eat foods with biotin, like eggs, nuts, and bananas. Drink lots of water.

# Best Daily Routine For Healthy Natural Nails

## Morning: Apply hand cream and cuticle oil.

## Evening: Remove makeup and dirt from under nails gently. Apply cuticle oil again.

## Weekly: File gently in one direction. Push back cuticles, do not cut them. Apply a nourishing base coat.

# What Not To Do For Healthy Nails

Do not bite your nails. Do not cut your cuticles deeply. Do not use your nails to remove gel. Do not keep polish on for more than 2 weeks without a break. Do not use harsh chemicals without gloves.

# Signs Your Nails Are Healthy

Healthy nails are smooth, pink, and flexible. They do not peel, split, or break easily. The cuticles are soft, not dry or torn. If your nails look like this, you are doing it right.

## Final Thoughts

Your natural nails can be long, strong, and beautiful - you just need to stop the 5 things that damage them. Stop peeling gel, stop using nails as tools, always use base coat and oil, file gently, and protect from water and chemicals.

If you follow these simple tips, your nails will become stronger, longer, and healthier in just a few weeks. Save this guide and share it with someone whose nails keep breaking!\\
