# What Are Pink Pearl Aura Nails?

Pink pearl aura nails are inspired by pink pearls, aura light, soft clouds, and romantic mornings. The base is a sheer soft pink with an aura effect - the center is a deeper pink that fades softly into a clear, milky edge, just like an aura glow.

On top, there is a shiny pearl finish that shifts from pink to white. Tiny pearls are placed in a curved line like a pearl necklace, with a clear flower and tiny crystals. Together, they look like tiny pink pearls on your nails.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Aura Base

Apply a sheer milky pink polish on all nails. With a sponge, dab a deeper pink in the center to create an aura effect. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Add Pearl Chrome

Rub white pearl chrome powder softly on all nails for a glowing pearl finish. Apply a top coat and cure.

## Step 4: Add Pearl Curve

Place tiny white pearls in a curved line near the cuticle on each nail with nail glue.

## Step 5: Add Crystal Flower

On the middle finger, place a clear 3D flower charm with a crystal center.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the pearls. Cure one last time. This will make the aura shine like glass.

# How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with pearls - do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a pearl feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the aura and pearls perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
