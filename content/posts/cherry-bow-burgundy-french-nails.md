Cherry nails are one of the most-saved nail art ideas on Pinterest, and this version takes them up a notch. **Glossy red cherries** with **tiny burgundy bows**, paired with deep **burgundy French tips** and little hearts, give a look that's flirty, romantic and seriously cute.

## What you'll need

- Base coat and glossy top coat
- Sheer nude or milky pink polish
- Burgundy polish
- Bright cherry-red polish
- A thin detail brush and a dotting tool

## Step 1: Nude base

Apply a base coat and one or two thin coats of sheer nude. This soft base makes the red details pop.

## Step 2: Burgundy French tips

On two or three nails, paint a **deep burgundy tip**. For almond nails, curve it into a soft V. Paint one nail fully burgundy as an accent if you like.

## Step 3: Paint the cherries

1. With a dotting tool, place **two round red dots** side by side.
2. Let them dry, then add a tiny **white dot** on each for a shiny highlight.
3. With a thin brush, draw two short stems that meet at the top.

## Step 4: Add the bows

Where the stems meet, paint a **tiny burgundy bow**: two small loops and two short tails. This is what makes the design feel coquette.

## Step 5: Little hearts and finish

Dot a few tiny burgundy **hearts** on the French tips, then seal everything with a glossy top coat.

## Make it easier

- Use **cherry nail stickers** and only hand-paint the bows.
- Skip the French tips for a minimal, everyday version.
- Add a **cherry ring** to complete the look.
