# What Are 3D Crystal French Nails?

3D crystal french nails are inspired by french manicures, crystals, diamonds, and fairy wings. The base is a soft nude pink, and the tip is not white - it is clear crystal, like glass and diamonds.

On top, there are 3D crystal butterflies that pop up from the nail, with thin silver lines and tiny crystals. Together, they look like your nails are made of crystal glass with butterflies resting on them.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Nude Pink Base

Apply a sheer nude pink polish on all nails. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Create Crystal French Tips

With clear builder gel, create the french tip. Add tiny silver glitter and small crystals inside the clear tip for a crystal effect. Cure.

## Step 4: Add 3D Butterflies

Place clear 3D crystal butterfly charms on each nail with nail glue. Press gently and hold for 10 seconds.

## Step 5: Add Silver Lines And Crystals

With a thin silver liner, draw delicate lines around the butterflies. Add tiny crystals on the tips.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the butterflies. Cure one last time. This will make the crystal shine like diamonds.

## How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with 3D butterflies - do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a charm feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel builder and 3D charms. Gel will keep the crystal perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
