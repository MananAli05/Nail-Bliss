If you want something different from pink and nude, but still soft and elegant, **sage green** is the shade to try. This muted, earthy green feels calm and natural, and tiny white flowers make it look like a spring garden on your fingertips.

## Why sage green is trending

Sage is a "quiet" color. It's less bright than mint and less dark than olive, so it pairs beautifully with cream knits, gold jewelry and neutral outfits. It's also a lovely way to try color if you usually wear nudes.

## What you'll need

- Base coat and glossy top coat
- Sage green polish
- White polish
- A dotting tool (or a bobby pin)
- A thin nail art brush
- Optional: gold striping polish or a gold gel pen

## How to paint the flowers

1. Apply a base coat and two thin coats of sage green. Let it dry fully.
2. With a dotting tool, place **five small white dots** in a circle to make petals.
3. Add a tiny **gold or yellow dot** in the center.
4. Use a thin brush to add a delicate gold stem or leaf if you like.
5. Seal with a top coat, being gentle so the flowers don't smudge.

**Tip:** you don't need flowers on every nail. Two accent nails per hand looks balanced and chic.

## Make it your own

- **Swap the flower color:** soft pink or lavender flowers look dreamy on sage.
- **Go minimal:** one tiny daisy near the cuticle on each nail.
- **Mix with nude:** alternate sage nails with milky nude nails.
