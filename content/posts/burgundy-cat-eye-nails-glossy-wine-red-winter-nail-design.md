Deep burgundy cat eye nails are the ultimate winter luxury. This rich wine red shade with a magnetic cat eye shimmer looks expensive, elegant and so festive.

Why You'll Love This Design

This is a deep burgundy shade with a glossy cat eye effect. The magnetic shimmer moves with the light and gives your nails a velvety, glowing finish. The almond shape makes your hands look long and elegant.

Perfect For

Christmas parties, winter weddings, date night, New Year celebrations and everyday glam looks. This color goes perfectly with gold jewelry, silk dresses and cozy winter outfits.

How To Get This Look

Start with clean, shaped almond nails. Apply a deep burgundy cat eye gel polish. Use a magnet to create the cat eye line in the center. Cure under UV light and seal with a glossy top coat for that glass-like shine.

Styling Tips

Pair these nails with a gold ring, a brown satin dress or a black outfit. Keep your makeup soft with nude lips to let your nails be the star.

If you love dark, classy and luxurious nails, burgundy cat eye nails are a must-try this winter.\\
