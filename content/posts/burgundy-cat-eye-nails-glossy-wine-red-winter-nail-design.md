## What Are Burgundy Cat Eye Nails?

Burgundy cat eye nails are nails painted with a deep burgundy shade that has a special magnetic shimmer in it.

Burgundy is a deep, rich red-wine color. It is a mix of dark red and a hint of purple and brown. It looks luxurious, warm, and classy. It is darker than red, softer than black, and richer than maroon.

Cat eye is not a color, it is an effect. Cat eye nail polish contains tiny magnetic particles. When you hold a magnet close to the wet polish, those particles move and form a bright, shimmering line or curve on the nail. This line looks exactly like a cat’s eye. As you move your hand, the light moves with it. That is why it is called cat eye.

When you combine deep burgundy with the cat eye effect, you get a manicure that looks like velvet, wine, and gemstone all at once. In normal light, it looks like a deep, glossy burgundy. In sunlight or party light, it glows with a beautiful magnetic shimmer.

## How Does The Cat Eye Effect Work?

Many people ask, how does cat eye polish create that beautiful line? The answer is simple, magnets.

Cat eye gel polish contains tiny metal particles that are invisible when the polish is in the bottle. When you apply the polish on your nail and hold a special cat eye magnet close to the nail for 10 to 15 seconds, those metal particles move towards the magnet and line up in a beautiful pattern.

Depending on how you hold the magnet, you can create a straight line, a diagonal line, a curve, or a velvet effect. In this photo, the magnet was held in the center, which created a bright, centered line that looks like a cat’s eye.

Once you cure the polish under a UV or LED lamp, the particles are locked in place. The effect stays until you remove the polish.

This is why cat eye nails look so different from normal polish. They have depth, dimension, and movement.

## How To Do Burgundy Cat Eye Nails At Home - Step By Step

You can do burgundy cat eye nails at home if you have gel polish and a UV or LED lamp. Here is the full step by step process.

## What You Need:

Burgundy cat eye gel polish, base coat gel, top coat gel, cat eye magnet, UV or LED lamp, nail file, cuticle pusher, cuticle oil, alcohol wipes

### Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Remove old polish. File your nails into a long almond shape like in the photo. Push back cuticles gently. Buff the nail surface very lightly. Clean nails with alcohol wipes to remove dust and oil. Good prep is the key to a long-lasting manicure.

### Step 2: Apply Base Coat

Apply a thin layer of base coat. Cure under UV or LED lamp for 60 seconds. Base coat protects your natural nails and helps polish stick better.

### Step 3: Apply First Coat Of Burgundy Cat Eye Polish

Apply a thin, even coat of burgundy cat eye gel polish. Do not cure yet. Hold the cat eye magnet close to the nail, about 5 mm away, for 10 to 15 seconds. Keep your hand steady. You will see the shimmer line form. Once you like the line, cure under lamp for 60 seconds.

### Step 4: Apply Second Coat For Deeper Color

Apply a second thin coat of burgundy cat eye polish for a deeper, richer color. Again, do not cure yet. Hold the magnet in the same position as before for 10 to 15 seconds to make the line stronger and brighter. Cure under lamp for 60 seconds.

This second coat is very important. It makes the burgundy look deep and the cat eye effect look sharp and clear.

### Step 5: Apply Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat. This adds shine and protects the cat eye effect. Cure under lamp for 60 seconds. Wipe with alcohol wipe to remove sticky layer if needed.

### Step 6: Apply Cuticle Oil

Massage cuticle oil around nails. This makes your manicure look fresh, healthy, and salon-perfect.

And done! Your burgundy cat eye nails are ready. They will look exactly like in the photo, deep, glossy, magnetic, and luxurious.

## Pro Tips For Perfect Burgundy Cat Eye Nails

1\. Always work on one nail at a time. Do not apply polish to all nails and then use magnet. The magnet only works when polish is wet.

2\. Hold the magnet steady. If your hand shakes, the line will become blurry.

3\. Keep the magnet close but do not touch the polish. If it touches, it will ruin the nail.

4\. Use 2 coats for best result. 1 coat looks light, 2 coats look deep and rich.

5\. Use a strong glossy top coat. Gloss makes cat eye shine more.

6\. If you want a softer effect, hold the magnet a little further away. If you want a sharper line, hold it closer.

## How To Make Burgundy Cat Eye Nails Last Longer

With gel polish, burgundy cat eye nails can last 2 to 3 weeks if you take good care of them.

Always use base coat and top coat. Wear gloves while washing dishes and cleaning. Do not use nails as tools to open cans or scratch. Apply cuticle oil daily. Apply top coat again after 7 days for extra shine. Avoid hot water for long time right after manicure. Do not peel off gel polish. Remove it properly with acetone and foil.

If you follow these tips, your burgundy cat eye nails will stay glossy and beautiful for weeks.
