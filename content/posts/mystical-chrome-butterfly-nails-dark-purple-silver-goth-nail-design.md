What Are Mystical Chrome Butterfly Nails?

Mystical chrome butterfly nails are inspired by dark fairy tales, midnight gardens, crystals, and moonlight. The base is a deep dark purple with a magical chrome shine that changes color in the light - sometimes purple, sometimes black, sometimes silver.

How To Get This Look At Home

Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

Step 2: Apply Dark Base

Apply a dark purple or black gel polish on all nails. Cure under UV light.

Step 3: Apply Chrome Powder

While the nails are still tacky, rub purple chrome powder over them with a sponge applicator. Buff until you get a mirror shine. Apply a top coat and cure.

Step 4: Add Butterflies

Place silver 3D butterfly charms on the thumb and pinky with nail glue. Add a tiny crystal in the center of each butterfly.

Step 5: Add Silver Chains And Stars

With a thin silver liner brush, draw delicate loops and chains on the other nails. Add tiny silver stars and crystals with nail glue.

Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the charms. Cure one last time for a glass-like shine.

Styling Tips For Mystical Butterfly Nails

Pair these nails with a black lace top, silver butterfly ring, and dark purple dress. Add silver earrings and a crystal necklace for the full mystical look.

For makeup, keep it dark and elegant. Smokey eyes, nude lips, glowing skin, and silver highlighter. This will match your nails perfectly.

For hair, sleek straight hair, soft waves, or a dark braid will look beautiful with these nails.

How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with 3D butterflies - do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a butterfly feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel chrome polish. Gel will keep the chrome shine perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
