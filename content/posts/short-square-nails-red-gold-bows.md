Not everyone wants long nails, and that's exactly why **short square nails** are so popular. They're strong, easy to live with and look incredibly neat. Add a little red, a little nude and some gold bows, and you have a manicure that's practical *and* romantic.

## Why choose a square shape?

- **The strongest shape.** A wide, flat tip spreads pressure evenly, so nails break less.
- **Great for short nails.** Square looks clean and intentional even at fingertip length.
- **Easy upkeep.** It's simple to file and quick to grow out.

If you'd like a softer version, gently round the corners. That's called **squoval**.

## How to file square nails

1. Hold the file straight across the tip, at a 90° angle to the nail.
2. File in one direction until the tip is flat.
3. File the sides straight, parallel to each other.
4. Lightly smooth the corners so they don't snag.

## Get the red & gold bow design

1. **Base:** apply a base coat, then glossy red on three nails and sheer nude on the rest.
2. **Red French:** on one nude nail, paint a thin red tip.
3. **Gold bows:** press small gold bow charms onto the red nails with gel or a dot of top coat.
4. **Details:** add a tiny red heart gem and a white 3D flower as accents.
5. **Seal:** cover everything with top coat, carefully around the charms.

## Why it works

Red and nude is a classic pairing. The gold bows add a coquette touch, while the short square shape keeps it wearable for everyday life. It's perfect for **Valentine's Day**, the holidays or any time you want a little romance.
