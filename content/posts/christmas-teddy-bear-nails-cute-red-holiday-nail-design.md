## How To Get This Exact Teddy Bear Manicure

If you want to show this to your nail artist, tell them these 5 points:

1\. Shape: Long almond, medium to long length.

2\. Base: Nude pink on bear and tree nails, deep red glitter on the rest.

3\. Hero Nail: Index finger with teddy bear in Santa hat holding a gift.

4\. Statement Nail: Middle finger with 3D red bow and pearl center.

5\. Detail Nails: Thumb and pinky with white snowflakes, ring finger with mini red Christmas tree and gold stars.

Ask for glossy top coat. Gloss is what makes the red glitter look wet and expensive.

## Can You Recreate It At Home?

**Yes, but you need patience.**

Start with clean, filed almond nails. Apply base coat, then nude pink on two nails and red glitter on three nails. Let them dry fully.

For the bear, use a thin brush and brown, black, white, and red polish. Draw a small oval face, two ears, two eyes, a nose, and a Santa hat on top. Add a tiny gift shape below. If drawing is hard, use a teddy bear sticker, it will still look adorable.

For the tree, draw a small red triangle, add a gold star on top, and tiny gold and red stars around it. For the snowflakes, use a white thin brush or a snowflake sticker.

For the bow, use a red 3D bow charm and stick it with nail glue. Add a pearl in the center.

Seal everything with top coat. Be gentle on the bow so it does not move.

**It takes time, but the result is worth it.**

## How To Keep Them Fresh All Through Christmas Week

Glitter nails last well, but 3D bows need care.

Wear gloves for dishes and cleaning. Do not pick at the bow or pearl. Apply cuticle oil every night. Add a fresh layer of top coat after 4 days. Avoid using nails to open boxes or cans.

With gel polish, this set can easily last 2 to 3 weeks. With regular polish, 7 to 10 days.

If the bow feels loose, add a tiny dot of nail glue under it and press for 10 seconds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is this teddy bear design good for Christmas parties?

Yes, it is perfect. It is cute, festive, and very photogenic. The red glitter and bow make it look dressed up, while the teddy bear keeps it sweet and fun. It works for family dinners, office parties, and holiday photos.

### Q2: Can I get this design on shorter nails?

You can, but keep it simpler. On short nails, choose the teddy bear on one nail, red glitter on others, and one snowflake nail. Skip the big 3D bow or use a smaller flat bow so it does not feel heavy.

### Q3: What if I cannot draw a teddy bear?

No problem. Use a teddy bear nail sticker or a water decal. They look clean and cute. Your nail artist can also hand-paint it. Stickers + glossy top coat look almost like hand-painted art in photos.

### Q4: Will the red bow fall off quickly?

Not if it is applied properly. A good nail glue + top coat around the edges keeps it secure for 2 weeks. Just avoid picking at it and wear gloves while cleaning. If it loosens, re-glue it with a small dot.

### Q5: What outfits match this manicure best?

Cream sweaters, red dresses, black coats, white knit sets, and plaid scarves all match perfectly. Gold jewelry, especially bow rings, makes the look even more festive. It is made for cozy winter outfits.
