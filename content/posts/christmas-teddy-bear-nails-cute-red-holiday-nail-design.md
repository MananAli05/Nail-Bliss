Christmas teddy bear nails are the cutest holiday nails this year. This red Christmas design has a teddy bear with a Santa hat, snowflakes, a shiny red bow and a tiny Christmas tree.

Why You'll Love This Design

Every nail has a different Christmas design. One nail has a cute teddy bear holding a gift, one has a red bow with a pearl, one has a Christmas tree and two have white snowflakes on glittery red. It is festive, cute and so perfect for Christmas photos.

Perfect For

Christmas parties, Christmas Eve, family dinners, holiday photos, gift exchange and winter vacations. These nails look amazing with a white sweater, red dress or cozy winter outfit.

How To Get This Look

Start with clean, shaped almond nails. Use red glitter polish for the snowflake nails, soft nude for the teddy bear and tree nails, and deep red for the bow nail. Add the teddy bear charm, bow charm and hand-painted snowflakes and tree. Finish with a glossy top coat.

Styling Tips

Pair these nails with a gold bow ring, a white knit sweater and red lipstick. These nails will be the star of all your Christmas pictures.

If you love cute, festive and cozy Christmas nails, this teddy bear design is a must-try this holiday season.\\
