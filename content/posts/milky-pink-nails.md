Some manicures are loud; **milky pink nails** whisper. This soft, semi-sheer pink makes nails look healthy, glossy and effortlessly polished, which is why it's the signature of the "clean girl" aesthetic. It's the manicure you can wear to a job interview, a wedding or a coffee run.

## What makes milky pink nails special

The secret is translucency. A milky pink polish has a soft white base that blurs the nail bed and hides uneven tips, while still letting a little natural color show through. The result looks fresh, not painted.

## How to get the look

1. **Shape:** almond or oval keeps it soft and feminine.
2. **Smooth the surface:** lightly buff ridges so the sheer polish looks even.
3. **Base coat:** a ridge-filling base is ideal for sheer shades.
4. **Two to three thin coats:** build the color slowly until you love the opacity.
5. **Glossy top coat:** milky nails are all about shine, so don't skip it.

## Tips for a perfect finish

- **Thin coats only.** Thick sheer polish can look streaky.
- **Healthy cuticles matter.** With a natural look, cuticles are on show, so use cuticle oil daily.
- **Keep nails the same length.** Even tips make the manicure look salon-done.

## Easy ways to dress it up

- Add **one tiny gem** or pearl near the cuticle.
- Paint a **micro French** with a thin white line.
