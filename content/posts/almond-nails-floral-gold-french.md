If you could only choose one nail shape forever, **almond** would be a safe bet. Slim at the sides and softly pointed at the tip, almond nails make fingers look longer, slimmer and more graceful on almost every hand.

## What is an almond nail?

An almond nail has straight or slightly tapered sides that curve into a rounded peak, just like the nut it's named after. It's softer than a stiletto and more elegant than a round nail.

## Who almond nails suit

- **Short or wide fingers:** the tapered tip draws the eye upward and adds length.
- **Long, slim fingers:** almond looks graceful and balanced.
- **Anyone who wants a feminine look:** it flatters both natural nails and extensions.

## How to file almond nails at home

1. Let your nails grow a little past your fingertip. Almond needs some free edge.
2. File both sides straight, angling slightly toward the center.
3. Gently round the tip into a soft point, filing **in one direction** to prevent splitting.
4. Check from above: both sides should look symmetrical.
5. Buff the edges smooth.

**Tip:** use a fine-grit file (180 or higher) on natural nails, because coarse files can cause peeling.

## Design idea: floral gold French

The almond nails in our photo are a perfect example of how this shape shows off detail:

- A **sheer nude base** keeps the look clean.
- **Thin gold lines** outline the French tip and follow the almond curve.
- **Tiny white flowers** with gold centers add a romantic touch.
- One **marble-white accent nail** ties everything together.
