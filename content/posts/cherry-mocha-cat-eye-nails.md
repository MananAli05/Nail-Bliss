**Cherry mocha** is the color of a cozy café on a cold day: rich brown with a deep cherry glow. As a **cat eye** manicure, it gets a velvety, shimmering finish, and little painted cherries make it playful and sweet.

## Why cherry mocha is the shade of the season

It sits perfectly between [burgundy](/nail-beauty/burgundy-wine-red-nails) and [chocolate brown](/nail-beauty/chocolate-brown-nails), so it's warm, flattering and ideal from early fall right through the holidays.

## How to get this design

1. **Base:** apply base gel and cure.
2. **Cat eye nails:** on the thumb and pinky, apply cherry-brown cat eye gel. Use a magnet to pull the shimmer into a soft glow, then cure.
3. **Sheer accent nails:** on the other nails, apply a **sheer brown or mocha jelly** polish so the nail looks like tinted glass.
4. **Cherries:** paint two small round cherries in deep red with thin brown stems. Add a white highlight dot on each.
5. **Sparkles:** add a tiny star or glitter dot beside each cherry.
6. **Top coat:** seal everything with high-shine top coat.

## Jelly vs. cat eye

| Finish | Look |
| --- | --- |
| Jelly | Sheer, glassy and see-through |
| Cat eye | Shimmery, velvety, with moving light |

Mixing both on one hand creates depth and makes the manicure look salon-made.

## Styling ideas

- Pair with **gold rings** and cozy knits.
- Keep the cherries to **two or three nails** for balance.
- Swap cherries for tiny **hearts** for Valentine's Day.
