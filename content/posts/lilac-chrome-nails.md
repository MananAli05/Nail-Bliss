If you love the glazed-donut trend but want something a little more magical, try **lilac chrome nails**. This soft lavender base with an iridescent pearl finish shifts between violet, pink and silver as you move your hands. It's subtle, dreamy and completely mesmerizing.

## Why lilac chrome works

- **Soft but special.** It's pastel enough for everyday wear but catches the eye in the light.
- **Seasonless.** Lilac feels fresh in spring, pretty in summer and frosty in winter.
- **Flattering.** The cool-toned shimmer brightens most skin tones.

## Two ways to get the look

### Option 1: Gel + chrome powder (salon finish)

1. Apply gel base and two coats of **sheer lilac** gel, curing each layer.
2. Apply a **no-wipe gel top coat** and cure.
3. Rub **white pearl or aurora chrome powder** onto the nail with a silicone tool or eyeshadow applicator.
4. Brush away the excess, then seal with another layer of no-wipe top coat.

### Option 2: Regular polish (no lamp)

Use a sheer lilac polish, then layer a **pearl or iridescent shimmer top coat**. It won't be as mirror-like as chrome powder, but it's beautiful and easy.

## Shape matters

Chrome looks best on **smooth, long shapes** where light can glide along the nail. Almond and oval are ideal. Read more in our [almond nails guide](/nail-shapes/almond-nails-floral-gold-french).

## Styling ideas

- Keep it **solid** on every nail for maximum shimmer.
- Add a single **silver star** or tiny pearl on the ring finger.
- Wear with **silver jewelry** to match the cool tones.
