**Cat eye nails** have taken over Pinterest, and for good reason. The magnetic shimmer creates a glowing band of light that looks like velvet or a gemstone. In a **dusty rose** shade with **burgundy butterfly** accents, it's romantic, rich and totally unique.

## What is cat eye polish?

Cat eye polish contains tiny **magnetic particles**. When you hold a magnet over the wet polish, the particles gather into a line or glow, creating that signature "cat's eye" shine. Most cat eye polishes are gels, but regular-polish versions exist too.

## How to do rose cat eye nails

1. **Prep and base:** shape nails into almond, apply a base and cure.
2. **Optional dark base:** a coat of black or deep red under cat eye makes the shimmer more dramatic.
3. **Cat eye layer:** apply one coat of rose cat eye gel.
4. **Use the magnet:** hold it close to the nail (without touching) for 5–10 seconds. Move it to the center for a stripe, or hold it at the side for a soft glow.
5. **Cure immediately**, then repeat for a second coat if needed.
6. **Top coat** for shine.

## Add the butterfly accents

On one or two **sheer nude** nails, paint or apply burgundy butterfly decals, then add a tiny gem in the center of each. The nude nails give the eye a resting place between the shimmery ones.

## Tips for the best shimmer

- **Work one nail at a time.** The particles move until cured.
- **Thin coats** give a sharper, brighter effect.
- **Experiment with magnet angles.** Each angle creates a different look.
