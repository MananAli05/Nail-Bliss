Opal glass nails are the most dreamy, elegant nails for brides and romantics. This design mixes milky white, iridescent opal shine, thin gold wire, tiny pearls, and a delicate crystal flower to create a look that feels like a piece of opal glass jewelry. It is soft, luxurious, and so magical. If you love bridal nails, pearls, and glassy shine, these nails are made for you.

What Are Opal Glass Nails?

Opal glass nails are inspired by opal stones, sea shells, glass, and fairy light. The base is a soft milky nude pink. On top, there is an iridescent opal film that changes color in the light - sometimes pink, sometimes blue, sometimes purple, sometimes gold.

Thin gold wire wraps around the nails like jewelry. Tiny pearls, rhinestones, and a 3D crystal flower sit on top like a crown. Together, they look like a piece of delicate glass art.

How To Get This Look At Home

Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a medium almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

Step 2: Apply Milky Base

Apply a milky white or sheer nude pink polish on all nails. Cure under UV light if using gel.

Step 3: Add Opal Film

Place small pieces of iridescent opal film or aurora powder on the nails. Press gently with a brush. This will create the rainbow opal shine.

Step 4: Add Gold Wire

With a thin gold wire or gold liner gel, draw delicate curved lines on each nail. Cure it.

Step 5: Add Pearls And Flower

Place tiny white pearls and rhinestones near the cuticle with nail glue. On the middle finger, place a 3D crystal flower charm.

Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy glass top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the opal shine like glass.

Styling Tips For Opal Glass Nails

Pair these nails with a white sheer dress, gold butterfly ring, and pearl earrings. Add a gold bracelet and soft white sweater for the full dreamy look.

For makeup, keep it soft and glowing. Nude lips, soft pink eyeshadow, glowing skin, and silver highlighter. This will match your nails perfectly.

For hair, soft waves, a low bun, or a braid with pearl clips will look beautiful with these nails.

How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with the 3D flower - do not pick at it. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a pearl feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the opal shine and pearls perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
