This design mixes juicy pink jelly, mirror chrome shine, tiny white hearts, clear butterflies, and delicate silver lines to create a look that feels like pink glass candy on your nails. # What Are Pink Jelly Chrome Nails?

Pink jelly chrome nails are inspired by pink jelly candy, glass, hearts, and fairy light. The base is a sheer juicy pink jelly that looks translucent, like pink glass. You can see a soft glow through the nail.

On top, there is a chrome finish that shines pink, lavender, and silver in the light. Tiny white hearts, clear butterflies, and thin silver lines complete the look. Together, they look like your nails are made of pink jelly glass with hearts inside.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Pink Jelly Base

Apply 2 thin coats of sheer pink jelly polish on all nails. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Add Chrome Powder

Rub pearl pink chrome powder softly on all nails for a glossy glass effect. Apply a top coat and cure.

## Step 4: Add Hearts And Butterflies

With white polish, draw tiny hearts. Place a clear butterfly charm on one nail with nail glue.

## Step 5: Add Silver Lines

With a thin silver liner, draw delicate lines around the hearts and butterflies.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully. Cure one last time. This will make the pink jelly shine like glass candy.

## How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the jelly shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel jelly polish. Gel will keep the pink jelly perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
