# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Cat Eye Base

Apply a strawberry milk pink cat eye gel polish on all nails. Do not cure yet.

## Step 3: Create Cat Eye Effect

Hold a cat eye magnet over each nail for 10 to 15 seconds. The magnet will pull the shimmer into a glowing line. Cure under UV light.

## Step 4: Paint Strawberries

With a thin brush, paint tiny pink strawberries with green leaves on each nail. Add tiny white dots for seeds.

## Step 5: Add Bow And Flowers

On the middle finger, place a clear pink bow charm with a pearl center. Add tiny white flower stickers on the other nails.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the cat eye shine like glass.

# How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with the bow charm - do not pick at it. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a charm feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel cat eye polish. Gel will keep the cat eye glow perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
