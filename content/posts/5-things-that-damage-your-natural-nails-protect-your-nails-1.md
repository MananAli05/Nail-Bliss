## 5 Things That Damage Your Natural Nails - Protect Your Nails

We all want long, strong, and shiny natural nails. But many times, without knowing, we do small things every day that slowly damage our nails. If your nails keep breaking, peeling, or feel weak, one of these 5 habits could be the reason.

The good news is, all of these are easy to fix. Let's look at the 5 most common things that damage your natural nails, and how to protect them.

## 1. Overusing Nail Polish

We love nail polish, but wearing it non-stop can weaken your nails. Frequent polish, especially gel and acrylic, doesn't let your nails breathe. The remover, especially acetone, dries out your nails and makes them thin and yellow.

What to do: Give your nails a break for 3-4 days between manicures. Use a base coat always. Use acetone-free remover when you can. Apply cuticle oil daily to keep nails hydrated.

## 2. Using Harsh Chemicals

Detergents, dish soap, bleach, and cleaning sprays are very harsh on nails. They strip natural oils from your nails and make them dry, brittle, and easy to break. If you clean without gloves, your nails suffer the most.

What to do: Always wear gloves while washing dishes or cleaning. Wash your hands with gentle soap, not harsh soap. After washing, apply hand cream and cuticle oil.

## 3. Nail Biting or Picking

Biting or picking your nails is one of the worst habits for nail health. It damages the nail bed, causes breaks, and can even cause infection around the cuticle. Picking off gel or acrylic polish also pulls off the top layer of your natural nail, making it thin and weak.

What to do: Never peel off gel or acrylic. Always soak it off properly or go to a salon. If you bite your nails, keep them short, apply bitter nail polish, and keep cuticle oil in your bag to keep your hands busy.

## 4. Skipping Moisturizer

Just like skin, your nails also need moisture. Dry nails break easily. If you skip hand cream and cuticle oil, your nails become dry, rough, and start peeling. Winter and cold weather make this even worse.

What to do: Apply hand cream 2-3 times a day. Apply cuticle oil every night before sleeping. Drink enough water. This one small habit will make your nails much stronger in 2 weeks.

## 5. Using Nails As Tools

Opening cans, scratching stickers, opening boxes, or scraping things with your nails is very damaging. Your nails are not tools. One wrong move can cause a painful break right from the nail bed.

What to do: Use a spoon, key, or opener instead of your nails. Keep a small tool in your bag. This will save your nails from sudden breaks.

## How To Keep Your Natural Nails Strong

If you want strong nails, follow these simple daily habits:

\- Use cuticle oil every night

\- Keep your nails clean and trimmed

\- Don't bite or pick your nails

\- Wear gloves while cleaning

\- Eat protein, biotin, and drink water

\- Take a break from polish every few weeks

You don't need expensive treatments. Just these small changes will make your nails longer, stronger, and healthier.

## Final Thoughts

Your natural nails can be beautiful and strong if you protect them. Stop these 5 damaging habits today, and you will see the difference in just a few weeks. Save this post, share it with a friend who loves nails, and start your healthy nail journey today!\\
