Coquette nails are all about soft pinks, delicate bows and a little bit of sparkle. They're romantic, playful and surprisingly easy to recreate at home. This step-by-step tutorial combines a classic **French tip** with tiny **pink bows** for a look that's cute enough for date night and subtle enough for every day.

## Tools and materials

You don't need a professional kit. Here's what you'll need:

- **Base coat**: protects your nails and helps polish stick
- **Sheer pink or nude polish**: the soft "your nails but better" base
- **White polish**: for the French tips
- **Pink polish**: for painting the bows
- **Rhinestones or nail stickers**: optional sparkle
- **Thin nail art brush**: a detail or striper brush works best
- **Bow stickers or 3D bow charms**: an easy shortcut if you'd rather not paint
- **Clear top coat**: to seal everything in

## Step 1: Prep your nails

Good prep makes nail art look neater and last longer.

1. Keep your nails clean and dry.
2. Gently push back your cuticles.
3. Shape your nails. **Almond** or oval looks the most coquette, but square works too.
4. Lightly buff the surface for a smooth finish.

Then apply a thin base coat and one or two sheer coats of pink or nude polish.

## Step 2: Create the French tip

- Start with your nude base fully dry.
- Use white polish or French tip guide stickers to paint a clean curve along the tip.
- Let it dry completely before you add any details.

**Pro tip:** for a softer, more modern look, try a "micro French" with a very thin white line.

## Step 3: Add the bow design

This is the fun part!

1. With a thin brush and pink polish, paint two small loops that meet in the middle. These are your bow wings.
2. Add a tiny dot in the center for the knot.
3. Draw two short tails hanging down.
4. Prefer no-fuss? Press on **bow stickers** or **3D bow charms** with a dot of top coat instead.
5. Add a little heart, pearl or gemstone for extra cuteness.

You don't need a bow on every nail. One or two accent nails per hand looks just as pretty.

## Step 4: Seal and finish

- Apply a clear top coat over everything, carefully sealing around any gems or charms.
- Cap the free edge of each nail to prevent chipping.
- Make sure everything is completely dry, and your cute coquette nails are ready!

## Extra tips for coquette nails

- **Keep your hands moisturized** so the soft, romantic look stays fresh.
- **Use cuticle oil daily** for a polished, salon-finish look.
- **Avoid harsh chemicals** that dull your top coat.
- **Wear gloves while cleaning** or doing household work.
- **Don't bite or pick** at charms; add a fresh top coat instead if one lifts.
- **Eat a balanced diet** with biotin and vitamins for stronger nails over time.
