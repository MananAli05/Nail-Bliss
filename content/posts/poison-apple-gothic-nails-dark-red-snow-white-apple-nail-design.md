What Are Poison Apple Gothic Nails?

Poison apple gothic nails are inspired by Snow White, the poison apple, dark fairy tales, and vintage storybooks. The base is a mix of deep blood red and soft nude pink. On top, there are tiny gold apples, red drips, gold bows, and a beautiful gold frame with a bitten apple inside.

Every nail tells a different part of the story. One nail is the poison apple, one is the dripping poison, one is the gold bow, and one is the vintage portrait. Together, they look like a dark, romantic fairy tale.

How To Get This Look At Home

Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

Step 2: Apply Base Colors

Apply deep blood red polish on thumb, middle, and ring finger. Apply soft nude pink on index and pinky. Cure under UV light if using gel.

Step 3: Add Apple Charms

On the middle finger, place a red apple charm with nail glue. On the ring finger, place a gold frame charm with a tiny apple inside.

Step 4: Create Dripping Tip

On the index finger, paint deep red drips from the tip downwards with a thin brush. Make them uneven for a natural dripping look. Cure it.

Step 5: Add Gold Bow And Stars

On the pinky finger, place a tiny gold bow charm. Add tiny gold stars and red dots on the nude nails with a dotting tool.

Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the red shine like glass.

How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Be gentle with apple charms - do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil every night. If a charm feels loose, add a tiny drop of nail glue.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the red and gold perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
