## What Are Burgundy Floral Nails?

Burgundy Floral Nails are inspired by vintage floral fabrics - soft nude backgrounds with deep red flowers, thin vines, and tiny gold details. It is floral, but not bright and summery. It is floral in a deep, moody, fall way.

The design in this photo features long almond nails with a sheer nude base. On each nail, there is a delicate hand-painted burgundy flower with thin brown vines and tiny leaves. In the center of each flower, there is a tiny crystal that sparkles like a dewdrop. The tips of the nails are finished in a deep glossy burgundy, like a modern French manicure but in a rich fall color.

Together, the sheer base, the burgundy flowers, and the deep red tips create a look that is soft, elegant, and absolutely stunning. It is not too loud, not too dark, and not too simple. It is the perfect balance.

## The Design Breakdown - What Makes This Look So Special

Let us look at each part of this design in detail so you know exactly what to ask your nail artist for.

## 1. The Shape - Long Almond

The nails in this photo are long almond shaped. Almond is one of the most flattering shapes because it makes your fingers look longer and slimmer. It is also perfect for floral designs because it gives a lot of space for the flowers and the French tips. If you prefer shorter nails, don't worry. This design also looks beautiful on short almond or oval nails. Just ask for smaller flowers and a thinner burgundy tip.

## 2. The Base Color - Sheer Nude

The base color is a sheer, soft nude pink. It is not a solid color, it is translucent like your natural nail but with a little bit of pink glow. This is what makes the design look so soft and natural. The nude base lets the burgundy flowers stand out without being too harsh. For the best result, ask for a sheer nude jelly polish with a glossy finish.

## 3. The Burgundy Flowers

The burgundy flowers are the star of this manicure. Each nail has one or two small flowers in deep burgundy red, with 5 delicate petals each. The flowers are hand-painted with thin vines and tiny leaves in brown and gold. Some flowers are fully open, some are still buds, which makes the design look like a real flower vine growing across your nails. In the center of each flower, there is a tiny crystal that adds sparkle.

## 4. The Burgundy French Tips

The tips of the nails are finished in a deep glossy burgundy. This is not a classic white French tip, it is a burgundy French tip. It is modern, elegant, and perfect for fall. The burgundy tip frames the floral design and makes the whole manicure look polished and finished. The tip is slightly curved and has a thin gold line where it meets the nude base, which adds a luxurious detail.

## 5. The Gold Details

Tiny gold lines, gold dots, and small gold beads are placed around the flowers and along the tips. The gold is very subtle, not too much, just enough to add warmth and luxury. It matches gold rings and gold jewelry perfectly and makes the manicure look expensive.

## How To Get This Look At The Salon

When you go to the salon, show your nail artist this exact photo. Tell them you want long almond nails, sheer nude base, hand-painted burgundy flowers with gold details, tiny crystals in the center, and deep burgundy French tips. A good nail artist will be able to hand-paint the flowers with a thin brush, or they can use small flower stickers or stamps if you want a simpler version.

If you want to make it last longer, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the shine for 2 to 3 weeks without chipping. Also ask for a strong top coat to protect the flower art.

## How To Recreate It At Home

If you love doing your nails at home, you can also try a simpler version of this look. You will need a sheer nude polish, a burgundy polish, a thin nail art brush, a dotting tool, small crystals, and a glossy top coat. Start with a base coat, then apply 2 coats of sheer nude. Use the thin brush to paint small burgundy flowers with 5 petals on each nail. Use the dotting tool to add a tiny crystal in the center. Then paint the tips burgundy. Finish with top coat. It takes a little patience, but the result is so worth it.

## Tips To Make Your Burgundy Floral Nails Last Longer

To keep your manicure looking fresh, always wear gloves when washing dishes, apply cuticle oil every day, avoid using your nails as tools, and reapply top coat every 4 to 5 days. This will keep the shine and protect the flower art.
