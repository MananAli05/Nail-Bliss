Icy lavender snowflake nails are the most elegant winter nails this year. This frosty design has lavender chrome shine, white snowflakes, silver glitter and tiny rhinestones.

Why You'll Love This Design

Every nail has a different frosty design. Two nails are shiny lavender chrome, two have white snowflakes on a soft pink base with glitter tips, and one is full silver glitter. The tiny rhinestones and sparkles make it look like fresh snow in the light.

Perfect For

Christmas parties, winter weddings, New Year celebrations, holiday photos and winter vacations. These nails look amazing with a white sweater, silver jewelry or a soft lavender outfit.

How To Get This Look

Start with clean, shaped almond nails. Use lavender chrome powder for the chrome nails, soft pink for the snowflake nails, and silver glitter for the glitter nail. Add hand-painted white snowflakes and tiny rhinestones. Finish with a glossy top coat.

Styling Tips

Pair these nails with a silver snowflake ring, a white knit sweater and soft glossy makeup. These nails will sparkle in all your winter pictures.

If you love soft, icy and elegant winter nails, this lavender snowflake design is a must-try this holiday season.\\
