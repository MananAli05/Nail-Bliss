## 6 Expensive Looking Rich Berry Nail Designs To Try

If you want nails that look rich, elegant, and expensive without being too loud, rich berry nails are the perfect choice.

Rich berry is a deep, dark, berry shade. It sits between burgundy, plum, and cherry. In daylight, it looks like a rich berry. In evening light, it looks deep and dark. That is why it is called expensive looking. It has depth, shine, and a luxurious feel.

In this article, we are sharing 6 stunning rich berry nail designs that are perfect for fall, winter, weddings, parties, and everyday glam. Each design is different. One is butterfly French, one is cat eye, one is bridal pearl, one is teal inspired, one is tortoise gold, and one is multi jewel.

Save your favourite design, show it to your nail tech, and get ready for compliments.

## Why Rich Berry Nails Look So Expensive

Rich berry nails are trending in the USA on Pinterest, Instagram, and TikTok because they look premium.

First, deep berry is a classic luxury colour. It is the same colour family that high end fashion brands use for bags, dresses, and lipsticks.

Second, rich berry suits all skin tones. On fair skin, it looks deep and romantic. On medium skin, it looks warm and glowing. On deep skin, it looks bold and royal.

Third, rich berry goes with almost everything. It looks beautiful with black dresses, white sweaters, gold jewelry, pearl rings, beige coats, and burgundy outfits.

Fourth, rich berry is perfect for every season, but it is especially beautiful in fall and winter. It looks cozy with sweaters, elegant with coats, and glamorous with evening dresses.

If you want a manicure that looks expensive in photos and in real life, rich berry is the best option.

## How To Make Your Rich Berry Manicure Last Longer

Follow these simple tips to keep your nails fresh for 2 to 3 weeks.

Always apply a good base coat and top coat. Do not use your nails as tools to open cans or boxes. Wear gloves while washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil daily to keep nails healthy. Avoid very hot water for a long time. Do not bite or pick your nails. Get a refill after 2 to 3 weeks if you have gel nails.

If you take care of your manicure, the shine and the jewel details will stay beautiful for a long time.

## DIY Vs Salon - Which Is Better For Rich Berry Nails

**DIY At Home**

You can try simple rich berry nails at home if you have rich berry gel polish, base coat, and top coat. You can use nail stickers for butterflies and French tips if you are a beginner.

DIY is good for simple designs like Design 5 and Design 1.

**Salon**

Salon is best for detailed designs like cat eye, 3D flowers, pearl work, and rhinestone work. A professional nail tech can create clean, detailed, and long lasting art.

If you want the designs in this article exactly like the photos, salon is the best option. Show the photo to your nail tech and ask for the same shape and finish.

### What To Ask Your Nail Tech

Copy this list and take it to your appointment.

I want rich berry colour, not pure dark, not bright red. I want glossy finish, not matte. I want almond or long shape. I want one or two accent nails with art, others simple. I want small crystals and pearls, not big stones. Please use gel polish for long lasting shine. Please seal the design with top coat.

This will help your nail tech understand exactly what you want.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1. What is rich berry nail colour?

Rich berry is a deep dark berry shade. It is a mix of burgundy, plum, and cherry. In daylight, it looks like a rich berry. In low light, it looks deep and dark.

### Q2. Are rich berry nails good for short nails?

Yes, rich berry looks beautiful on short nails too. French, tortoise, and simple glossy rich berry designs are best for short nails. They make short nails look neat and elegant.

### Q3. How long do rich berry gel nails last?

Rich berry gel nails usually last 2 to 3 weeks with proper care. Regular polish lasts 5 to 7 days.

### Q4. Can I wear rich berry nails to work?

Yes, absolutely. Choose simple designs like tortoise gold French or butterfly French for work. Avoid too many jewels and big crystals for a professional look.

### Q5. Are rich berry nails good for weddings?

Yes, rich berry is one of the best colours for weddings. It looks royal with both Eastern and Western bridal dresses. Butterfly French and bridal pearl flower designs are perfect for brides.

### Q6. What dress colour matches rich berry nails?

Rich berry nails match with black, burgundy, maroon, white, cream, beige, gold, ivory, and blush pink dresses. It is a very versatile colour.
