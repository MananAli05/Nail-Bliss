If there's one nail color that always looks elegant, it's **burgundy**. This deep wine red sits somewhere between classic red and dark berry, so it feels sophisticated without being as bold as fire-engine red. It's a favorite for fall and winter, but honestly, it works all year.

## Why burgundy is a forever shade

- **Flattering on every skin tone.** The blue-red undertone makes hands look brighter and more even.
- **Looks polished on any length.** Short and square or long and almond, burgundy always looks intentional.
- **Instantly dressy.** It turns a simple outfit into a "put-together" look.

## Steps for a perfect burgundy manicure

1. File nails into a smooth almond or oval shape for a feminine finish.
2. Apply a base coat. Dark reds can stain, so don't skip this step.
3. Paint two thin coats, keeping polish slightly away from the cuticle for a clean line.
4. Clean up edges with a small brush dipped in remover.
5. Finish with a glossy top coat for that rich, jewel-like shine.

## Ways to style burgundy nails

- **Date night:** all-over glossy burgundy with a thin gold ring.
- **Office:** short, rounded burgundy nails look classic and professional.
- **Holidays:** add a gold foil or glitter accent nail.
- **Romantic:** pair burgundy French tips with tiny cherries, like our [cherry bow nail art](/nail-art-ideas/cherry-bow-burgundy-french-nails).

## Burgundy shades to try

| Shade | Look |
| --- | --- |
| Wine | Deep red with purple undertones |
| Oxblood | Dark red-brown, very moody |
| Berry | Brighter and slightly pink |
| Cherry mocha | Burgundy with a brown, cat-eye shimmer |

## Keep it chip-free

Dark shades make every chip visible. Reapply top coat every few days, avoid hot water right after painting and keep cuticles moisturized.
