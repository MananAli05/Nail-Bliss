# Liquid Gold Chrome Nails - Molten Gold French Nail Design

Liquid gold chrome nails are the most luxurious, elegant nails for gold lovers. This design mixes soft nude pink, molten liquid gold, and tiny crystal stars to create a look that feels like melted gold dripping on your nails. It is shiny, expensive, and so glamorous. If you love gold, chrome, and luxury, these nails are made for you.

## What Are Liquid Gold Chrome Nails?

Liquid gold chrome nails are inspired by melted gold, liquid metal, jewelry, and champagne. The base is a soft sheer nude pink that looks natural and clean. The tips are not a normal French tip - they are molten gold, wavy and dripping like liquid metal.

On top, there are tiny gold lines, crystal stars, and small rhinestones near the cuticle. They look like tiny diamonds floating in gold. Together, they look like your nails were dipped in pure gold.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Nude Base

Apply a sheer nude pink polish on all nails. Cure under UV light if using gel.

## Step 3: Create Molten Gold Tips

With a thin brush, paint wavy, dripping gold chrome gel on the tips. Make the edge uneven and drippy like melted gold. Cure it.

## Step 4: Add Gold Lines

With a thin gold liner, draw delicate wavy gold lines from the cuticle down to the gold tip.

## Step 5: Add Crystal Stars

Place tiny crystal stars and small rhinestones near the cuticle with nail glue. Add a few gold dots around them.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the gold shine like a mirror.

# How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the gold shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel chrome polish. Gel will keep the gold perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
