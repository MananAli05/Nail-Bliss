Autumn maple leaf nails are the most elegant, luxurious nails for fall. This design mixes soft nude, warm caramel brown French tips, golden maple leaves, and tiny gold glitter to create a look that feels like golden autumn leaves falling on a cozy evening. If you love fall, gold, and elegant nails, these nails are made for you.

What Are Autumn Maple Leaf Nails?

Autumn maple leaf nails are inspired by golden maple trees, fall leaves, candles, and cozy sweaters. The base is a sheer soft nude pink. The tips are a warm caramel brown. On top, there are delicate golden maple leaves with tiny crystals and gold glitter lines.

Every nail has a different leaf design, but together they look like a beautiful autumn forest. It is classy, warm, and so luxurious.

How To Get This Look At Home

Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

Step 2: Apply Nude Base

Apply a sheer nude pink polish on all nails. Cure under UV light if using gel.

Step 3: Create Brown French Tips

With a thin brush, paint a caramel brown French tip on each nail. Keep the tip soft and curved. Cure it.

Step 4: Add Maple Leaves

Place golden maple leaf stickers or hand-paint maple leaves with gold polish. Place one big leaf on the middle nails, and small leaves on the others.

Step 5: Add Gold Glitter And Crystals

Add thin gold glitter lines around the leaves. Place tiny gold crystals near the leaves with nail glue.

Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the gold leaves shine like glass.

Styling Tips For Maple Leaf Nails

Pair these nails with a cream knit sweater, gold leaf ring, and warm gold jewelry. A brown or beige handbag will complete the cozy autumn look.

For makeup, keep it warm. Nude lips, warm brown eyeshadow, soft blush, and glowing skin. Let your nails be the luxury detail.

For hair, soft waves, a low bun, or a braid with a gold leaf clip will look beautiful with these nails.

How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the nails shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days to keep the gold shiny.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the leaves and glitter perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
