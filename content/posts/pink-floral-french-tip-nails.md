Classic French tips are elegant on their own, but add a sprinkle of tiny **pink blossoms** and they become pure romance. This design looks detailed, yet it's built from simple dots, perfect if you're new to nail art.

## What you'll need

- Base coat and top coat
- Sheer pink or nude polish
- White polish (for the tips)
- Pink polish in two shades (light and medium)
- Green polish (for leaves)
- A dotting tool and a thin detail brush

## Step 1: Create the base

Apply a base coat, then one or two coats of sheer pink. Let it dry completely so your art doesn't drag.

## Step 2: Paint the French tips

Paint a soft white curve along the tip of each nail. On almond nails, follow the natural curve so it meets in a gentle point. Not confident freehand? Use French tip guide stickers.

## Step 3: Add the flowers

1. With a dotting tool, place **five small light-pink dots** in a circle on the white tip.
2. Add a **darker pink dot** in the center.
3. Scatter a few more flowers along the smile line, letting some overlap onto the nude base.
4. Keep them small. Tiny flowers look more delicate.

## Step 4: Leaves and finishing

With your thinnest brush, add tiny green leaf strokes beside some flowers. Let everything dry, then seal with a glossy top coat.

## Tips for beginners

- **Wipe your dotting tool** between flowers so dots stay neat.
- **Odd numbers look natural.** Try three flowers on one nail and two on the next.
- **Practice on paper** or a nail wheel first.

## Make it yours

- Swap pink for **lavender or peach** flowers.
- Paint just the **accent nails** for a minimal version.
- Try the same flowers on a [sage green base](/nail-beauty/sage-green-flower-nails).
