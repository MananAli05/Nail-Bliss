Black magnetic velvet nails are the most luxurious, moody, and elegant nails for dark glam lovers. # What Are Black Magnetic Velvet Nails?



Black magnetic velvet nails are inspired by black velvet, dark nights, stars, and silver jewelry. The base is a deep black with a magnetic velvet effect - a soft, glowing shimmer that moves like velvet fabric when you move your hand.



On top, there are thin silver starburst lines and tiny crystals placed like diamonds on velvet. Together, they look like your nails are made of black velvet with stars sparkling inside.

# How To Get This Look At Home



## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.



## Step 2: Apply Black Base

Apply a deep black magnetic gel polish on all nails. Do not cure yet.



## Step 3: Create Velvet Effect

Hold a cat eye magnet over each nail for 10 to 15 seconds. Move it slowly to create a velvet glow. Cure under UV light.



## Step 4: Add Silver Starburst

With a thin silver liner, draw a starburst on one nail. Place a tiny crystal in the center.



## Step 5: Add Crystals

Place tiny crystals on the tips of the other nails with nail glue.



## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully. Cure one last time. This will make the black velvet shine like glass.

## How To Make Them Last Longer



Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the velvet shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days.



If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel magnetic polish. Gel will keep the black velvet perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
