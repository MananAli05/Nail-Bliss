# Rusty Copper Chrome Nails - Molten Copper Metal Nail Design

Rusty copper chrome nails are the most warm, bold, and luxurious nails for fall lovers. This design mixes deep rusty copper, molten metal shine, cracked texture, and tiny crystals to create a look that feels like melted copper metal on your nails. It is warm, edgy, and so glamorous. If you love copper, chrome, and fall vibes, these nails are made for you.

# What Are Rusty Copper Chrome Nails?

Rusty copper chrome nails are inspired by molten copper, rust, autumn leaves, warm candles, and vintage metal. The base is a deep rusty brown-copper with a mirror chrome finish. It looks like liquid metal.

On top, there is a cracked texture like broken glass or cracked earth, with thin gold lines and tiny crystals. Together, they look like your nails are made of molten copper jewelry.

# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Copper Base

Apply a deep rusty copper gel polish on all nails. Cure under UV light.

## Step 3: Add Chrome Powder

Rub copper chrome powder on all nails for a mirror metal effect. Apply a top coat and cure.

## Step 4: Create Cracked Texture

With a thin black-brown liner, draw thin cracked lines across each nail. Make them random like cracked glass.

## Step 5: Add Gold Lines And Crystals

With a thin gold liner, trace over some cracks. Add tiny crystals near the cuticle.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails. Cure one last time. This will make the copper shine like a mirror.

# How To Make Them Last Longer

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the copper shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days.

If you get them done at a salon, ask for gel chrome polish. Gel will keep the copper perfect for 3 to 4 weeks.
