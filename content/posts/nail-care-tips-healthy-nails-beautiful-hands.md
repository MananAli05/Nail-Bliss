Healthy nails, beautiful hands, always. The prettiest manicures start with strong, well-cared-for nails, and a sprinkle of creativity. In this guide you'll find **four dreamy nail art ideas** you can try at home, followed by the **simple nail care habits** that keep every manicure looking its best.

## 1. Coquette bow nail art

Soft, romantic and endlessly cute, bow nails are one of the most-saved looks on Pinterest.

- Use a thin nail art brush or stickers to create the bow.
- Keep the base color soft: pink, nude or clear.
- Add a little heart or pearl for extra cuteness.

Want the full walk-through? Follow our step-by-step [coquette bow nail art tutorial](/nail-care-tips/coquette-bow-nail-art-tutorial).

## 2. Cherry nail art

Cherry nails are flirty, fun and perfect for summer or Valentine's Day.

- Use a deep red or cherry shade for a rich look.
- Add small cherry details with a fine nail art brush: two red dots, a thin stem and a tiny green leaf.
- Seal with a top coat for long-lasting shine.

**Styling idea:** alternate full cherry-red nails with sheer nude accent nails featuring a single cherry.

## 3. Floral French tip nails

A fresh twist on the classic French manicure.

- Start with a nude base.
- Create French tips with white or pastel polish.
- Add tiny flowers using a dotting tool or brush: five small dots in a circle with a contrasting center.

This design looks especially lovely on almond or oval nails, two of the [best nail shapes for short fingers](/nail-care-tips/best-nail-shapes-for-short-fingers).

## 4. Butterfly nail art

Dreamy, delicate and perfect for spring.

- Use butterfly stickers or hand-paint them with a thin brush.
- Try a pastel or nude base color for a dreamy look.
- Finish with a glossy top coat.

## Nail care habits for healthy nails

Gorgeous nail art only looks its best on healthy nails. These everyday habits make a real difference.

### How to make your manicure last longer

- Always use a base coat, your polish, then a top coat.
- Avoid too much water. Hot water can weaken polish.
- Wear gloves while cleaning or doing household work.

For a complete routine, read [how to prevent nail polish from chipping](/nail-care-tips/how-to-prevent-nail-polish-chipping).

### How to care for natural nails

- Keep your nails clean and dry.
- Moisturize your cuticles daily.
- Use a nail strengthener or nourishing oil.

### Best nail shapes for short fingers

- **Oval and almond** shapes make fingers look longer.
- Avoid very square shapes if you want a slimmer look.
- **Squoval** is a great middle ground if your nails break easily.

### How to prevent polish from chipping

- Apply thin layers.
- Don't forget the top coat.
- Let each layer dry properly.
- Avoid using your nails as tools.

## Extra tips

- Keep your hands moisturized.
- Eat a balanced diet with biotin and vitamins.
- Avoid harsh chemicals.
- Give your nails a break from polish sometimes.
- Be gentle, and don't pick or bite your nails.

> Take care of your nails… they're part of your beauty story.

## The bottom line

Pick one of these four nail art ideas for your next manicure, and build in a couple of the healthy-nail habits above. Your nails will look prettier *and* grow stronger. Healthy nails are always in style!
