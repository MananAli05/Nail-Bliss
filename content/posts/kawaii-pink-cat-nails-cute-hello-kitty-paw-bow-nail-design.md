What Are Kawaii Pink Cat Nails?

Kawaii means cute in Japanese. Kawaii nails are all about soft colors, cute characters, tiny charms, and sparkly details. This design is the perfect example.

The base is a soft blush pink with clear glitter tips. On top, there is a cute Hello Kitty face with a pink bow, three pink paw prints, and tiny bows and hearts. Every nail is different, but together they look like a cute little cat story.

How To Get This Look At Home

Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long coffin shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

Step 2: Apply Pink Base

Apply a soft blush pink gel polish on all nails. For the tips, add clear glitter polish to create a sparkly ombre effect. Cure under UV light.

Step 3: Add Hello Kitty

On the index finger, paint or stick a Hello Kitty decal. Add a tiny pink bow on her ear with nail glue.

Step 4: Add Paw Prints

On three nails, place 3D pink paw charms. Use nail glue to secure them. Make sure they are centered and straight.

Step 5: Add Bows And Hearts

Add a tiny pink bow charm on the pinky finger. Place tiny silver hearts and rhinestones around all nails with a dotting tool.
