## What Are 3D Floral Jelly Nails?

3D Floral Jelly Nails combine two of the biggest nail trends - jelly nails and 3D flower art. Jelly nails are sheer, see-through nails that look like tinted glass or jelly. They are soft, glossy, and very dreamy. They are not a solid color, they are translucent, so you can see a little bit of your natural nail through them.

On top of that jelly base, this design adds beautiful 3D flowers. The flowers are not flat or painted, they are raised up from the nail, so you can actually feel them. They look like real tiny flowers made from gel. The flowers in this photo are in soft pink and white shades, with tiny crystal centers, pearls, and gold leaves around them.

Together, the clear jelly base and the 3D flowers create a look that is soft, romantic, and absolutely magical. It looks like a tiny flower garden preserved in glass.

## The Design Breakdown - What Makes This Look So Special

Let us look at each part of this design in detail so you know exactly what to ask your nail artist for.

## 1. The Shape - Long Almond

The nails in this photo are long almond shaped. Almond is one of the most flattering shapes because it makes your fingers look longer and slimmer. It is also perfect for floral designs because it gives a lot of space for the 3D flowers to sit beautifully. If you prefer shorter nails, don't worry. This design also looks beautiful on short almond or oval nails. Just ask for smaller 3D flowers so they don't feel too heavy.

## 2. The Base Color - Clear Pink Jelly

The base color is a clear, sheer pink jelly. It is not a solid pink, it is translucent like pink glass. This is what makes jelly nails so special - they look glossy, juicy, and dreamy. The pink is very soft, like blush pink or cherry blossom pink. For the best result, ask for a sheer pink jelly gel polish with a high-gloss finish.

## 3. The 3D Flowers

The 3D flowers are the star of this manicure. Each nail has one or two 3D flowers in the center. The flowers are in soft pink and white shades, with 5 delicate petals each. They are made from 3D sculpting gel, so they are raised and look like real flowers. Some flowers are bigger, some are smaller, but every nail has that same garden feel.

## 4. The Pearls and Crystals

Tiny white pearls and clear crystals are placed around the flowers. The pearls look like tiny dewdrops on petals, and the crystals sparkle like morning light. In the center of each flower, there is a tiny crystal or pearl that makes the flower look like real jewelry. This detail makes the whole manicure look expensive and luxurious.

## 5. The Gold Leaves and Details

Tiny gold leaves, thin gold lines, and small gold beads are placed around the flowers. The gold looks like delicate vines and stems. It ties the whole design together and makes it look like a piece of floral jewelry. It matches gold rings and gold bracelets perfectly.

## How To Get This Look At The Salon

When you go to the salon, show your nail artist this exact photo. Tell them you want long almond nails, clear pink jelly gel polish, and 3D pink and white flowers with pearls and gold leaves on each nail. A good nail artist will use 3D sculpting gel to create the flowers, then place the pearls and crystals with nail glue and seal everything with top coat.

If you want to make it last longer, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the shine for 2 to 3 weeks without chipping. Also ask for a strong top coat around the flowers to keep them secure, but be careful not to flatten them.

## How To Recreate It At Home

If you love doing your nails at home, you can also try a simpler version of this look. You will need a sheer pink jelly polish, 3D flower charms or stickers, small pearls, gold nail stickers, and a glossy top coat. Start with a base coat, then apply 2 coats of jelly polish. Then glue one 3D flower charm in the center of each nail. Add tiny pearls and gold leaves around it. Finish with top coat around the flowers, being careful not to cover them completely. It takes a little patience, but the result is so worth it.

## Tips To Make Your 3D Floral Jelly Nails Last Longer

To keep your manicure looking fresh, always wear gloves when washing dishes, apply cuticle oil every day, avoid using your nails as tools, and be gentle with the 3D flowers. Do not pick at them. Reapply top coat around the flowers every 4 to 5 days to keep them secure. This will keep the shine and protect the art.
