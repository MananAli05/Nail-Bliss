## The Design Breakdown - What Makes This Look So Special

Let us look at each part of this design in detail so you know exactly what to ask your nail artist for.

## 1. The Shape - Long Almond

The nails in this photo are long almond shaped. Almond is one of the most flattering shapes because it makes your fingers look longer and slimmer. It is also perfect for bow designs because it gives a lot of space for the 3D bows to sit beautifully. If you prefer shorter nails, don't worry. This design also looks beautiful on short almond or oval nails. Just ask for smaller 3D bows so they don't feel too heavy.

## 2. The Base Color - Soft Pink Jelly

The base color is a soft, sheer pink jelly. It is not a solid pink, it is translucent like pink glass. This is what makes jelly nails so special - they look glossy, juicy, and dreamy. The pink is very soft, like blush pink or baby pink. For the best result, ask for a sheer pink jelly gel polish with a high-gloss finish.

## 3. The Big 3D Bow

The big 3D bow is the star of this manicure. It is on the middle nail, in the center. The bow is made from glossy pink gel, with two big loops and two long tails. In the center of the bow, there is a tiny silver crystal that sparkles like a diamond. The bow is raised up from the nail, so you can actually feel it. It looks like a real satin ribbon tied on your nail.

## 4. The Small 3D Bows

There are also two smaller 3D bows - one on the thumb nail at the tip, and one on the pinky nail at the tip. These bows are smaller and more delicate, but they still have that same glossy pink look. They make the whole set feel balanced, like every nail has a little gift on it.

## 5. The Hand-Painted Bow

One nail has a flat, hand-painted white bow. The bow is drawn with thin white lines, with a tiny crystal in the center. This nail is different from the others because it is flat, not 3D. This contrast makes the set more interesting. It shows that you can mix flat art and 3D art in one manicure.

## 6. The French Tips, Pearls, and Crystals

The tips of the nails are finished in soft pink French tips, which makes the manicure look polished and finished. Tiny pearls, crystals, and a tiny heart charm are scattered across the nails. The pearls look like tiny dewdrops, and the crystals sparkle like diamonds. These details make the whole manicure look expensive and luxurious.

## How To Get This Look At The Salon

When you go to the salon, show your nail artist this exact photo. Tell them you want long almond nails, soft pink jelly base, soft pink French tips, one big 3D pink bow, two small 3D bows, one hand-painted white bow, and tiny pearls and crystals. A good nail artist will use 3D sculpting gel to create the bows, then place the pearls and crystals with nail glue and seal everything with top coat.

If you want to make it last longer, ask for gel polish. Gel will keep the shine for 2 to 3 weeks without chipping. Also ask for a strong top coat around the bows to keep them secure.

## How To Recreate It At Home

If you love doing your nails at home, you can try a simpler version. You will need a soft pink jelly polish, 3D bow charms or stickers, small pearls, crystals, and top coat. Start with a base coat, then apply 2 coats of jelly polish. Paint the tips soft pink. Glue one big bow charm in the center of one nail and smaller bows on the other nails. Add tiny pearls and crystals around them. Finish with top coat around the bows. It takes patience, but the result is so worth it.

## Tips To Make Your 3D Coquette Bow Nails Last Longer

To keep your manicure looking fresh, always wear gloves when washing dishes, apply cuticle oil every day, avoid using your nails as tools, and be gentle with the 3D bows. Do not pick at them. Reapply top coat around the bows every 4 to 5 days to keep them secure.
