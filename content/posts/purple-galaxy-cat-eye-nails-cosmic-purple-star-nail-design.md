# How To Get This Look At Home

## Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Start with clean nails. Shape them into a long almond shape. Push back cuticles and apply a clear base coat.

## Step 2: Apply Purple Base

Apply a deep purple cat eye gel polish on all nails. Do not cure yet.

## Step 3: Create Cat Eye Effect

Hold a cat eye magnet over each nail for 10 to 15 seconds. The magnet will pull the shimmer into a glowing galaxy line. Cure under UV light.

## Step 4: Add Galaxy Shimmer

Rub galaxy chrome powder softly on all nails for a cosmic shimmer. Apply a top coat and cure.

## Step 5: Add Stars And Crystals

Place tiny silver stars and small crystals on each nail with nail glue.

## Step 6: Seal With Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat carefully around the stars. Cure one last time. This will make the galaxy shine like glass.

Always wear gloves when washing dishes. Apply cuticle oil every night to keep the galaxy shiny. Avoid using your nails as tools. Get a top coat refresh after 10 days.
