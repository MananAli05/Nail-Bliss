## How To Do Candy Cane Christmas Nails At Home - Step By Step

You can do candy cane Christmas nails at home if you have patience and the right tools. Here is the full process for this exact design.

## What You Need:

Soft pink nude polish, red polish, white polish, thin nail art brush, dotting tool, top coat, base coat, small pearls, red bow charms or red polish for bows, snowflake stickers or white polish for snowflakes, cuticle oil

### Step 1: Prep Your Nails

Remove old polish. File your nails into a long almond shape like in the photo. Push back cuticles gently. Clean nails well. Apply base coat and let it dry.

### Step 2: Apply Soft Pink Base

Apply 2 coats of soft pink nude polish on all nails. This is the base for the whole design. Let each coat dry completely. The pink base makes the design look soft and feminine.

### Step 3: Create Candy Cane French Tips

This is the main step. On each nail, use a thin brush to draw diagonal red and white stripes on the tip. Start with white as the base of the tip, then add red diagonal stripes on top. Make the stripes thin and neat. Let it dry.

For a cleaner look, you can use French tip guides first, then add red stripes on the white tip.

### Step 4: Add Snowflakes

On the thumb, index, and pinky nails, draw a white snowflake in the center of the pink base. Use a thin brush or a snowflake sticker. Add 6 small lines from the center, like a star. Add tiny dots around it for sparkle. Let it dry.

### Step 5: Add The Red Bow

On the middle finger, draw or stick a red bow in the center. If you are using polish, draw two triangles for the bow and a small circle in the center. If you are using a 3D charm, stick it with nail glue. Add a small white pearl in the center of the bow. This is the highlight of the set.

### Step 6: Add The Tiny Candy Cane

On the ring finger, draw a tiny candy cane in the center with red and white stripes. Use a thin brush. Draw a curved hook shape. Add a small red bow on it. Add tiny white dots around it.

### Step 7: Apply Top Coat

Apply a glossy top coat on all nails to seal the design and add shine. This also protects the bows and pearls. Let it dry completely.

### Step 8: Apply Cuticle Oil

Massage cuticle oil around nails. This makes your manicure look fresh and salon-perfect.

And done! Your candy cane Christmas nails are ready.

## Pro Tips For Perfect Candy Cane Christmas Nails

1\. Use a thin brush for stripes. Thick stripes will look messy.

2\. Let each layer dry before adding the next one. If you rush, the colors will mix.

3\. Use stickers for snowflakes if you cannot draw them. Stickers look just as beautiful.

4\. Use nail glue for 3D bows and pearls, so they do not fall off.

5\. Always seal with top coat. Top coat makes the design last longer and look glossy.

6\. Work in good light. Small details need good light.

## How To Make Candy Cane Christmas Nails Last Longer

With gel polish, candy cane Christmas nails can last 2 to 3 weeks. With regular polish, they can last 7 to 10 days.

Always use base coat and top coat. Wear gloves while washing dishes and cleaning. Do not use nails as tools. Be careful with bows and pearls, do not pick at them. Apply cuticle oil daily. Apply top coat again after 4 to 5 days for extra protection. Do not peel off polish.

**If a bow or pearl falls off, you can stick it back with a small dot of nail glue and top coat.**
